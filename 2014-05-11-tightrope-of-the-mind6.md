We walked the tightrope with bare feet
Our hands held forward with barbed wires upon our wrists
Flames licked our sole, heat from beneath
Shed sweat sheets from equal heat within
But even all our blood, sweat and other wet
Could not quell the fire, stop our death
But the scenery is staged and only the fear is real
Imaginings of a mind rid of will
