What are these demons that haunt me?
These shadows that are not mine
Yet sprout from me
I see, but I am unable to understand
Touch, but unable feel
Hear, but unable to listen
Smell, but unable to percieve
Tongue, unable to taste
What kind of life is this
What kind of life do I live
I must put an end it
But how do you kill a life, that is not alive
