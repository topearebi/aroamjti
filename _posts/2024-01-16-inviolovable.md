To love is to violate your very own soul.
To reach down into yourself
And contort your being
Make grooves into your skin
To fit against the edges of someone else's person.
And to smile if it hurt 
To smile especially when it hurts
To love is to test yourself
To hold your breath
Under water
In the middle of the sea
So you can never scream
No matter how much the pressure builds
Inside your chest
Love is swallowing a gulp of water
With each gasp of air
It floods your lungs
With life, 
and something that reminds you pain
In every organ
And every cell
So hold your breath
Under water
In the middle of the sea
Till you're strong enough
To love again
To scream
And breathe
And drown a little bit
With each inhale
Because it's beautiful
Under the sea
