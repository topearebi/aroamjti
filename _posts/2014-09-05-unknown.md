We are all little twisted and deformed creatures
But after a while. When we've spent time with our deformity
We begin to revel in it. Take joy from it
Maybe we aren't so bad after all
Maybe, just maybe, we can blend in with normalcy
With the normals
If we spend enough time among them, maybe we'll pass for one of them
So we spend enough time
And we start to want things
Things that should not be attainable to us, but we begin to feel we deserve
We feel entitled
So we ignore truth
And we start to feel...like we belong
Like we're....normal
