date: 2012-03-20 10:00:00 +0000

She kissed me
So I kissed her back
Or did I kiss her because I already desperately wanted to?
I couldn't tell at the moment
I had lost all reason
And also lost all doubt
Her flames licked at my tongue
Scorching my mouth
And yet I licked at her flames
Fanning them with my own wetness
Till they had all but consumed me
And I was charred and burnt inside
None would ever recognise me again
I had been reborn in her heat
Transmuted into a different man
By a most base alchemy
The alchemy of a kiss
Never to be the same again
For I made out with fire
