### Revereri. Sacrificium

The thing with discovering yourself much later in life is that you have spent much time accumulating all these bits and pieces that now no longer fit. Friends, family, dreams, and career aspirations you no longer aspire to. Your mistakes seem even more glaring when you see how far away they have taken you from where you want to be.

Left unchecked, we may even start to believe that everything we are, and every choice we have made up to that point, has been a mistake. Nothing we possess now can help us on the path we see so clearly.

And yet we must remember that we would not have found this path without these now seemingly useless things. It does us no good to despise the darkness through which we stumbled to find our light; to detest the scars that we accumulate stumbling through the forest to find our path. Rather, we must accept that these things are necessary and responsible for the clarity we now possess.

It is up to us to find the strength to unburden ourselves of the weight of this history, begin this new journey with purpose, and with reverence in our hearts for the sacrifices we will make for it.
