"Sometimes home isn't 4 walls, it's 2 eyes and a heartbeat"
- You're my home. (via sativa-mermaid)

My warmth comes not from the sun
But from your arms, wrapping around my form
My bed is soft
But I would rather lay my head in your bosom
There's a rhythm to your walk
Your every sentence is a song
Bidding me come,
Welcome
Sometimes home is not 4 walls
But 3 days with you

I miss you Snookums
