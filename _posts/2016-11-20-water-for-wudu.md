date: 2016-11-20 12:00:00 +0000

i.
You are everything that my hands want to ever hold
Like water for wu'du
Ridding me of my tiny imperfections

ii.
I am rubbing you against my skin
You sting where you seep into old wounds
With absolution comes the pain of healing

iii.
Perhaps I should have you scrape these old pieces of me away
Perhaps tayammum is what my body needs to become pure again
For I am thirsty, and there is not enough water to unparch me
