Gold like gold.
White like stone.
Gold like god.
White like snow.
Gold like the sun.
White the sun.
Gold like your skin when you were born.
White like your nails, your eyes, your teeth.
Like a just bloomed flower trapped in time.
You will never wither in my eyes
