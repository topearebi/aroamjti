I've been reading the 5am club and cheesy as it sounds, I'm not about to knock anything that proposes to give me more control over my day/life.

Here's the kicker though: I wake up 5am everyday anyways. Except for days when I sleep by 5am.

Anyways. I spent last week practicing. Now I've included the 1 hour block that this extra time is supposed to buy me. I did an interval workout this morning and boy oh boy. It really drove home how strenght, power and endurance are very different things. I've been increasing my PRs for many lifts but dynamic movements for 40 seconds just nearly took my life this morning. And by the end of it, I started feeling good.

Today's a Monday, and as much as I don't believe in the concept of new days and weeks et cetera, I do understand the concept of dread as a routine and work worries me. While I do not dread it yet, I do realise I have to pull off something pretty great to get back on track.

Today, create a timeline. We already have timelines for work, but an ad-hoc timeline with all these dates that keep popping up might be helpful.

Update my competency question bank. At one question a day, I owe myself about 2 weeks worth now. Not ideal.

Redraft and rewrite all my articles. I need a paper trail of work in public. A good place to start is fixing old content if new content is proving... difficult to get through.

Well then. That's all he wrote.
