In the foetal position
Curled up against the wall I was
Silently shedding silent tears
The pain in my heart, long since dulled by the pain upon my skin
Only a dull throbbing remained, in both places
I raised my forearm to my face
To once more stare at my self done tattoo
Newly acquired
To dull the pain
Like I always did
It was still fresh
The blood only now starting to leak
1 word. 5 letters
Carved into cold flesh
Mimicking a cold heart
I had proof now that this is what I felt
What I truly was
My tattoo was a physical expression
Of an abstract emotion
Now everyone would see that I wasn't crazy
I had proof
I had the word "ALONE" carved on my arm
