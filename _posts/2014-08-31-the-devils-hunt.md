Ripped jeans
Drunken teens
Exposed skin
Time to sin
Bright lights
Dark stair flights
Gangs and groupies
Hos and floozies
Alcohol induced
Alcohol infused
Wanton behaviours
Giving away of favours
Night time
Nothing to rhyme
The devil is loose
He's come to choose
Please do say thanks
If you're of his ranks
There is no greater honour;
If you have a brain tumour
You stayed out late
Blame but your fate
That you've been caught
In his rose, thorn fraught
And while you stare
Brain stuck in fear
his elements of night
Take you in flight
You're already his
With this make peace
There is no escape
From this night landscape
He'll take just you
Just all of you
Till tonight is through
Then tomorrow too
