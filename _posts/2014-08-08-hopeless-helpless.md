They come in pieces, this flock
Each is different yet they're all the same stock
They smell dissimilar.
Taste peculiar
But feel the same
Beneath my fingers they're all a game
They all respond
Giving limited freedom
At first
Though eventually
We know they'll break free
Of the restraints society
Placed on we
Humanity
Now knowing the facts
Your eyes clear of its cataracts
Pass judgement thus
Are they bland or am I numb?
Are they sin or are they sun?
Am I loon or am I lost?
