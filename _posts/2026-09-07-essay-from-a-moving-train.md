Essays from a moving train
—-
For some reason I feel like I am starting from zero. Like I don’t know how to write. Not like I forgot, but like I never knew how. I am trying to pull on a limb that isn’t there. Not a now missing one. Rent from me by a traumatic experience or just general disuse, but rather a third arm or leg that I never had but for some reason really believed that I did until this very moment that I now have to draw it.
It feels ludicrous to think I ever could. Like dejavu feels convincing. “This must have happened before”. But if you were to ever try to convince anybody that it dd. Really did. Beyond just the sense that you have that it did. Well, they would find you amusing wouldn’t they? Why ever would you think that the world really is some sort of specific localised loop that only you experienced. Why did I ever think I could write?

I suppose this isn’t really about writing. It is more about the height of my opinion of myself. A symptom of a damaged self-esteem. The purple bruising seeping into my thoughts like the pigment off a watercolour brush.

I suppose this isn’t really about watercolour. Rather it is about love and its meaning. The conditions under which it perseveres. Or should persevere. Sometimes love starts to feel like a phantom limb. Not in that it is now lost, but maybe it was never truly there.
