date: 2021-08-18 23:00:00 +0000

A few years ago, I used to imagine I'd do everything myself. Money? Yeah I'll make it myself. A job? Na I'm good by myself. Love? Pfft. I'm fine all by myself. More and more however, I am learning that one of the key ingredients to success is collaboration. It's why founders have co-founders and why silent partners and angel investors and all these things exist. You can't just do it yourself.

Well you can, but your accomplishments will be limited. Each person's threshold for how far they can go it alone is different, but limited nonetheless. You need both collaboration and competition to exhume your full potential.

So now if I'm offered money, I'll take it, I'll use it, and make a mental note to pay it back at some point.

I also spent 3 hours in traffic and avoided the gym because it's leg day. Lagos traffic is enough.
