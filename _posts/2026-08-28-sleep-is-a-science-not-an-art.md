Last night, I slept well. That's notable because usually, I don't. For a long time I've told myself it's habit. I'm wired that way. Which is probably still valid. I've been waking up at 5am since I was 8 years old. My first year of boarding school. I was terribly afraid of peeing myself in bed and so I just conditioned myself to wake up on time to pee. And the rest is history.

Lately however, it has been significantly worse. I barely fall asleep till 2 pm, and I still wake up at 5am, tired, groggy and still with a ton of work to do. Which means even all the extra time I get from not sleeping early isn't productive. I don't get more done in a day than I would otherwise. Suck it 5 day work week.

What is interesting after last night though is that, after weeks of telling myself the phone has nothing to do with it because I have had nights where I have stayed up hours without touching a phone, I turned it off at 9:30 like my calendar said to, switched to my Kobo to read 1 chapter of Piranesi, barely, and promptly fell asleep by around 11. There were a few hiccups and regressions, but I managed it.

I also woke up not at all stressed. I'm usually a morning person anyway, but there was just a little less brain fog. I did a tiny little workout, did the dishes (note to self, do dishes at 9:30 next time) and made it in time to work to write this little journal about last night.

So yeah, the blue light thing is probably bad.
