I'm quite terrible at socialisation. Coupled with my issues with consistency, I am rather terrible at maintaining social relationships. Apparently relationships require nurturing, for some people anyways. 

I read an article describing how some people don't have proper friendship degradation mechanisms. The summary of which seemed to be that for some people, friendships remain in the same status since the last interaction. Even when significants lengths of time pass between interactions and that is so me.

Personally, I think it's not that I don't like people, I simply just assume that we like each other based off of our understanding of who we are as people not how many memes per day we both find hilarious.

This is a problem, for obvious reasons, chief of which is that I have terrible platonic-social relationships.

I am considering a dedicated social day, preferably Saturdays every week where I make time to go through my contact list and burn pretty much all of my social energy by calling every single person that I would like to maintain contact with.

I saw an interview with Michael [Bublé](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=2bUfUN9wUrs&si=uPe9Mir8G_7vvH0-) here where he talks about the importance of connection and more than the things he says, its the way he says them that has me moved. I want to be able to speak in that manner about the people in my life
