Maybe if I wasn’t a writer I wouldn’t have lost you
They say us poets love the most, the hardest we let ourselves get too lost in what other people feel
I tried to become you to write you and maybe I lost myself too much in everything that you were and who really wants to be in a relationship with a reflection of themselves?

- A.A
