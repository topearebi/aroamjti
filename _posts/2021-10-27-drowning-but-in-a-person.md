I was going through my notes a while back like I tend to when I have the blues and I was surprised to find that I don’t entirely hate everything I’ve written. And perhaps i found it touching because it reminded me that even terrible things can feel good when you are with someone.

I stand naked before the sea

It whispers your name as it's fingers brush against my skin and It says to me

we must honour all that used to exist within me

Before it takes me

The sea is the personification of memory and you are all that my skin cells remember

My bones aches at the thought of seeing you again

Albeit as a series of thoughts and images

I read once that everything we feel is the product of electricity flowing through our brains

I am hard pressed to agree 

The way I see it

If feelings were electricity 

What I feel for you would have long since consumed me

And perhaps it has

But the water cleanses me
