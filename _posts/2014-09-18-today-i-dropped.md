Today I dropped
A couple of miles before my stop
Just for the extra walk

Longer periods without talk
This is the little I ask for 
For all I give in return

I should probably run
I can’t afford to miss the 8 o’clock class I’m going for
It’s already 7:54

There’s so many people I’m at the door
Right now I don’t remember what I go to school for
Everyday I’m here like clockwork

Pass my exams or not
I’ll probably still end up poor
There’s too many people that know 2 + 2 is 4

I am nothing but a sore
An eyesore a bed sore
And now I’m just sore

-me walking to class way too early in the morning
