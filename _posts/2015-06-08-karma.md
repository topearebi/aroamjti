Karma doesn’t care about the date
It’ll hunt you through time and space

So watch your back and clean up your act
Or you might find yourself needing the devil’s pact

Seeking protection
From spiritual retribution

But even then ye may not be safe
In the depths of hell you shall meet your fate

For karma’s long hands
May reach far into the devil’s lands

Such is the way of nature
And so beware the present to secure your future
