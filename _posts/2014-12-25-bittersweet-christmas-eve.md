It’s christmas eve and I can barely fall asleep. Even with the pills. The fireworks sound in the distance they’re killing me, I can’t think. In the house they sing, they’re singing. Celebrating. And all I can think about is how christmas blurs the lines between my room and the outside world. Santa might as well kick my door in. I picture the fat fuck and imagine asking him what he brought me. A pair of smokes or a pack of socks. I know which one I’d want. I’m thinking why they bother when they know he isn’t real. Christmas was ruined for me at age 6 when I realized Santa couldn’t possibly know everything about me. What gifts to get me. But they’re family anyways so I go out to celebrate with. I take my bottle with me. 24 year old ronin, I take a swig. Christmas decorations are nothing compared to what I see. Alcohol is a better reward for being good I think. Now I’m standing under the christmas tree. The room got quieter when I walked in. Even my mother is quiet it seems. The kids too notice this, all eyes are on me. Briefly I think how christmas has given me the attention I need. I’m drawn to my other gift. Right on the floor is a postcard. There’s a bow wrapped around it. Its got words that show its addressed to me. I pick it up and a flash of jealousy almost overhwelms me. Santa could get everyone’s big boxes through the nonexistent chimney but me? I understand the reason for their looks. I turn the card over and I see, the words that were addressed to me, my gift; “You were born for this. You were born to live.” My eyes tear up. Its the worst gift, it is. Its exactly what I need. But what this means…this means…santa might be real…but if he is, I got nothing from my family…
