date: 2017-06-12 12:00:00 +0000

You will be fine someday.
Not today, but someday
Today is the time to hurt
the time to cry, maybe, if you know
how to let yourself do such a thing
But someday, maybe even tomorrow,
you will be fine
Everything will be alright
But today, you hurt
today, I hurt
