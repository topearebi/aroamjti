I would worship the very earth you walk on do you see?
Do you see?
The way I touch that book right as you set it down
Fold up your clothes as you lay them out
There isn't a part of you that I could bear to part with
Do you see?
The way I am quiet when you walk into the room
Like I do not want to miss even the sound of your breath
I am still as I can be
Tense
Like a coiled spring
I am waiting for a chink in your armour I think
Some opening in your defenses that I can tear through
I am frightened. Do you see?
Your walls might be too high to scale for me
And I have fragile limbs
Brittle bones from all my broken dreams
I do not want to wake up to see
All the pieces of me
Drifting away, like dust at your feet
