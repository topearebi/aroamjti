I have walked in shadows, in valleys, amidst graves in cemeteries
But nothing prepared me for a feast like this
This table of knowledge and forbidden bread
Like manna from hell; enlightenment
Is this temptation or a test of faith
The voice of God, or the noise of the silver tongued one
It is torture to live like this
Torture to live in-between
I need to know of what it is I sip
Is this blood or the kool-aid of faith
All you need is to believe they say
As they press this oil to my forehead
And baptize my tongue in religion
Now in the light I walk
But why then, does everything seem so dark
