They requested she wear white
Tho she couldn't fathom why
They said that's how weddings go
She felt it was a lie
So she gathered her tools, in order to set things right
White represents virginity
A concept alien to her
Ever since she was raped an uncle
When she was but a new turn'd teenager
So she took a little blood, and spread it on
White represents purity
A concept she was strangers with
Her soul, tainted, continuously blackened
In process non stop, infinite
So she took a little muck, and spread it on
White represents innocence
An idea separated from her head
Chief of the cardinal sins,
Envy, had already to her wed
So she took a little mould, and spread it on
