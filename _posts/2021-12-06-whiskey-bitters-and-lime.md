I can not help but wonder if there is some sort of hidden knowledge at the end of this glass.

The liquid is viscous. The contents swirl in a state that suggests (i) they are not yet a perfect mix or (ii) they are open the gateway to some mystical land.

Either way, I shall have to drink myself to the answer.
