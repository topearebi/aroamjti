And with my eyes I do see
Those which do not exist...
....Should not exist
These demons that haunt me
Dogging my every step
Hanging on to me like dark to night
Devouring any that tries to touch me
These demons I created
These demons, merely acting on my wishes
Yet I do not want them
They terrify me
These spawn of mine
Spawned by my mind
Or maybe they aren't
Maybe they did exist apart from me
Only latching on to me for the darkness I spew
Parasites feeding off my misery
Whichever it is
Whatever it is
I hate them
These monsters I now refer to as SHADOWS IN SPACE
