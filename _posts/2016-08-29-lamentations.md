In the beginning was the word
From which everything else was born
And the word was God
Who fashioned man from dust
In his own likeness

And dust, to dust shall return
But Lord, all I see is blood
I have read the books
And taken you at your word
But Lord, these bodies do not shatter like earthenware pots when they fall

Ashes to ashes but you left out the stench
The air is corralled with the smell of human flesh
There is nothing godly about despair
Us 7 billion gods are devouring ourselves

Lord, if you be at all, are you scared?
Perhaps even you did not foresee this end
Please send us another son to die in our stead
If I blaspheme rage over my sin, send me to hell
And I shall wail, 'this one valued the lives of his fellow men'

Lord, if you be at all, how long will you be silent?
Are you not the very earth itself?
Gaia herself would stir to witness such wretchedness
But Most Benevolent, we do not so much as get a thundercloud
Or a pillar of salt in revenge

Lord, if you be at all, how do you compare?
What must we bring to the scales to garner your help?
We offer up to you our
Syria
Pakistan
Gaza
Northern Nigeria
Racism
Genocide
Apartheid
Crib death
Yet they do not match up to your Sodom and Gomorrah, do not spark your interest

Lord, if you be at all, be dead
That we may know someday all of this world will end
