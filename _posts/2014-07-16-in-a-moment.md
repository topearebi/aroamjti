She came
Walking in my direction
Towards me
Just that knowledge made my heart beat a little faster
I shook it off
And collected myself
Acting nonchalant
I sat. Trying to keep calm
Trying to act indifferent
But then she was here
I perceived her now familiar scent, before I saw her
Felt her, before she touched me
I looked up
And looked upon her
And my resolve broke
And all the things I felt
And everything I wanted to say
Rushed to the tip of my tongue
Yet I could not speak them
Those words, that would lay bare my feelings
and strip my heart of the defences I built
Defences it took me all my life to build
About to crumble, in one instant
It was definitely wrong
It felt definitely right
Bitter-Sweet
Sweetly bitter
This girl was dangerous
