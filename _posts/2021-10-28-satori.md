“It’s my life too”

The words kept ringing in my head long after they had been said. Like a flash of inspiration. Like finding actual words of wisdom in a self-help book and realising that the answer to your problem is simple. Because the answers to all problems are simple and the exact same thing. You have to do something about it.

Change is not instantaneous. It is not a linear progression towards a goal. Nothing is. Not the stock market, not human evolution, not human behaviour. Change is built in little increments. Ups, downs and periods of stagnation. It is not one book that changes your life, it is the sum total of everything you’ve read, everything you have learnt and lived building up to a crescendo. Like building sand pyramids, each grain stacks up till it reaches max load, then overload, then it crashes. Rinse. Repeat. But always, the castle is growing.

We can take control of this growth, this assimilation. We can live more, experience more, learn more. Some of this information will be lost to us, some buried under the weight of all that we are. All that we are becoming. But we must choose our grains of sand. Choose the building blocks of our castles. Or life will choose for us. Other people will choose for us. And someday, when our castle is complete, we will look around us, and we will recognise nothing.
