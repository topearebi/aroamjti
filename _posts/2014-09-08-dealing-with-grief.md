I remember before. A time not so long ago. In a place not so far from here. Where we lived. Sorry, where our love lived. Though I'm not so sure anymore if it was real love. Really love.

Now I know this is cliché but looking back on it the signs were always there. You never did delight in the things I did. You never lost yourself in the feelings like I did. The joy never took over you like it did me. I guess its really my fault though, I must have been selfish. I was so busy widening my own smile I never noticed that yours didn't quite touch your eyes.

You only let out enough to fool me. I should have known. Should have seen it, but I was too caught it up in our happiness. Our happiness? Haha. I can see now you were never truly happy with me.

I probably made it so easy for u to leave me. I noticed everything about you. Wiped the smudges of your lipstick. Moved your hair out of your face and all the things that lovers do. Turns out I was never really paying attention. Maybe I'm the one in the wrong here, maybe I'm the criminal. So caught up as I was in what I thought love should be I musta took everything for granted.

I guess I should thank you very much for teaching me. That love isn't really a certain thing or set of things, It could be all about misery. Which is what I'm certain your gonna find in the arms of that other guy. Not that I blame you cos it was probably I who cheated first. I thought I understood you, and sometimes I still think I do. Now I know though that a female can never really be understood. Its probably wrong to call all females thus but right now I'm too numb to care about being politically correct.

I should have known that what you want isn't happiness or caring. You don't want to be doted on and cherished.

You don't want to share ice-cream or watch the skies. Or listen to me gush about how I love your eyes. You don't want to walk a thousand miles hand-in-hand. Or lay down for hours, with your back in the sand.

Now don't get me wrong, she's not a masochist. She's just wants life too fast and I'm not about that. She wants really fast cars. Holidays on private islands. Dinner at expensive restaurants not take out eaten on the palm of our hands. Fine wine and vintage things. Shiny rings to match her teeth.

So here I sit in apartment 13. Its 7 o'clock in the morning and I'm writing this note so full of sorrow. True to form like I've done every other morn. When I'm done I'm rip it apart. Just like that thing you did with my heart. And thus I once more outline my plan of grief;

I'll put on my mask and walk down the stairs. Smile at the super while breaking down inside. I'll get our ice-cream from around the block. I'll buy two cones, just like we used to do. By the time I'm finished with one, and yours is melted, I'll stare at it for a while before setting it down. In that corner in the alley where we shared many kisses. I'll put it next to all the other ones. Then reflect on how much the pile has grown. I'll wonder why no-one has come to take them. Maybe that'll get me frustrated and never do this again. Instead the universe taunts me with it. Keeps the pile safe to remind me of my misery.

Then I'll walk to the beach, like I did yesterday. And the day before that, and the day before. It really just goes on and on. Maybe this time, I'll see you there. My heart almost starts to beat again, with a flicker of hope. But quick as a blink the flicker's died. I don't want to see you again my love. I don't want to see what you've become. Have you flourished, have you bloomed? Perhaps even more than I always knew you would. I don't think my heart could bear it, seeing you once more. The excitement might start it up once more. Its been so long since my heart beat though. I fear with all the stretch marks it wouldn't make it past 4. Funny, 4 used to be our favourite number. Ever since it won you that teddy bear. Now it'll be number that spells my death. If my heart should beat 4 times I'd die.

Forgive me my redundancy my love. But a heart so broken should be allowed at least that much. Now that I've written it out in words, I really don't think I can take that much. The sorrow is really too much to bear. I think I'll just lay back in bed like I've been doing since last year
