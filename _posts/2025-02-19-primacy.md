You come first. Always.

Well, I should always come first to me. Not my interests of fancies. Or even if they do, it should be in a way that their value to me is put first before anything else.

What should this look like in practice?

My passions. My obsessions. They should be things that make me feel good. Better. Not things I pursue by helping them feel better. By this I mean people. Fall in love with people who are in love with you. Who are committed to your pleasure. Your peace. Your service. Only then is your service valuable.

Will this act help you sleep better? Heal better? Work better? Or does it just help someone else feel better

Will this effort be entirely wasted?

Have some consideration today.

And astrology isn't real. Don't forget that.
