I gazed upon it
But I did not see
So I gazed even harder
Yet notin
It was empty
Full of nothing
This was impossible
So I touched it
And it rippled....calmly
But I did not feel anitin, but cold dead air
It was clearly disturbed
The object obviously solid
But yet, permeable
And so I stepped in
Stepped into the black abyss
With its dead air
And it accepted me
And when I was in
And I breathed in the dead air
I felt more alive than I had ever been
And I walked in it
Its fabric was soft
Like I was being smothered by feathers
A feeling indescribable
Her heart was nothing like I had expected beforehand
Like nothing I had experienced before
This was a different type of good
Not the usual colours and roses you would expect
This was BEAUTIFUL
And it was BLACK
