For the past couple of weeks, I have done little of the things that I truly want to do. This is not to say that I am unhappy or unfulfilled because of it. My unhappiness and lack of fulfilment are more an existential crisis that seems insurmountable and yet for some reason, I find myself hoping that doing the things that I think I should or need to do will be instrumental in surmounting this obstacle.

I am aware that some type of psychopathology is involved in my inability to balance my desire and my actions. I would regret the set of choices or actions and inactions that led me to this condition if I could for the life of me figure out what those choices are. For all my supposed objectivity, I seem incapable of seeing myself clearly. Which is nothing particularly terrible unless, like me, you are looking to cure this condition.

I have been preparing for IELTS as well as taking a course in Product Management which I hope is the start of a career change. But as if to balance out this new attempt at productivity I have also resumed the bad habit of obsessively reading wuxia stories.

It is a strange experience to be constantly hindered by yourself when you attempt to, at least in your opinion, do better. To be an interloper inside of your own body, sharing it with an Other who seems determined to persist in things that you consider antithetical to your goals.

To be anxious about results yet too depressed to take action. It is amazing the things we will judge harshly in others yet unknowingly give into ourselves. I am anxious about the results my efforts will yield. Anxious that the results I am capable of will not be up ti par and yet while the obvious solution would be to double the amount of effort or at least apply it more efficiently, the other in me, to which I am othered, vehemently insists that I read a book with two thousand chapters, driven by a shoddy plot of which I have now read a thousand other times. Yet, I cannot seem to stop.

I am aware that there are potential solutions such as removing the external factors that cause me to be distracted. I could delete the file I am currently stuck on but I know that is only a temporary measure. I already have a bunch of others just waiting for me to get stuck on, In fact, I have managed to convince myself that the way out is through. It would be faster to just finish the book.

**IELTS, WES and other matters of international standing**

The idea of having to write an English exam is so infinitely strange to me. I have spoken the English Language all of my life and studied it formally for almost half that and yet I am required to write a test to prove that I can use it in conversation. Surely it is some sort of cosmic jest.

After a couple of months and a few hundreds of thousands of naira, the body known as World Education Services has created a great and powerful document telling me that my master's degree, is in fact a master's degree. Now I am not usually one to whine about the practices of foreign bodies and nations but clearly, these things are made-up barriers designed to exploit people who dare to aim above their standing.

Of course, ultimately, it is my fault for living in a shitty country and not being exceptional enough to be granted the rights to waive these things
