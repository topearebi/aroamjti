The sun is setting
Such a beautiful setting
A lovers setting

The stage is set
For lovers yet
To love and pet

Rolling in grass
To one another harass
One another's carcass

Kisses on the upper lip
Kisses on the lower lip
To go even lower still to sip

And each lover takes turns wearing their partner as crown
Upon their crown
Each unable to frown

And when all is done
And the light is gone
Lovers have had their fun

In each other's arms they mourn
The setting of the sun
And the end of their fun
