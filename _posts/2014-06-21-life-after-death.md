At times
I sit around and think:
What is the point of anything?
If it all ends in death

So what is it that keeps me going?
That mysterious force that drives me

Is it the thought of an afterlife?
That eternity and forever after?

But wouldn't even that get boring?
After a while we'd all be snoring

Living a life of perfection
With no room for improvement or evolution

Eventually our minds would wither away
Our intelligence, decay

Then we would once more fall in vain
Probably never to be born again
