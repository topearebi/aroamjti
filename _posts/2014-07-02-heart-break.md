Here once lay the heart of a young man pleading, for someone to stop its bleeding, because it had been broken, by words spoken, by the lips of a young woman, who was in love with another man. 

The whore caught cheating, was given a beating, but still could not feel even a fraction of the young man's pain, which was continuous, unrelentless and heavy as stormy rain. 

The man was driven to insanity, by the woman's vanity. Driven over the edge, he died and remained still as a hedge. At the end of this story, we can see that in love there is no glory
