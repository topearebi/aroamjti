Why does man need a creator?
We are constantly haunted by an inferiority complex
Some say we were made from chaos
And merely exist by chance, or happenstance
Others say we came from order
A perfect system, created by a supreme being
But none believe that we just came into existence
Like the chaos did
Like the supreme being did
