Write their name in blood
After first having mixed it with mud
Let it dry and cake
But a short while it will take
Let it for all time represent
The taint in their bloodline, ever present
Those made of dirt and desire
Ambitious, always aiming higher
Daring to reach above their station
Adhering to the laws of neither God nor Nation
They we shall strike down
Amputate their limbs and split their crown
For we hate them for having what we do not
Freedom to live, think, and so they must rot

- On the persecution of free-thinking individuals
