Sometimes I wish
You weren't so big
Or so bright
That I was the only thing
You could hold in your orbit

Perhaps thats a little selfish
Or a little greedy of me
To want to be
The only planet in your system

Everything with life
Or the potential for it
Seems drawn to you

I do not want to keep
Going round in circles

Consume me that I may be the fuel that keeps you burning
