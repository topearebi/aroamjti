It's evening and I'm still in my gym clothes. My gym bottoms anyways. And shoes. And yes as you can guess, it means I haven't had a bath since I got back.

A favourite hobby of mine is to psychoanalyse little quirks and give them largely unrelated or exaggerated interpretations a la personality traits.

For this, I have decided that the reason I do this, and I do this a lot, is that I have trouble letting go of things that make me feel good. Perhaps I am so starved of positivity or anything that is generally good that when I find something that provides me with some measure of happiness, I become terribly reluctant to let it go. Hence the reason why I am writing today in compression pants.

My body is almost at where it used to be earlier in the year, but it is nowhere near where I would like for it to be. Also, this ginger guy I have a crush on gave me a great tip for my lat pulldowns that made the workout really burn today and I will be eternally grateful.
