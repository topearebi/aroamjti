I've been binging again. Food. Sleep. Netflix. And I know. I know it’s bad for me. For my health and my life but it’s about all I can manage right now. And it’s a lot better than my usual coping mechanisms. Oh boy. Thank God for that.

Someone asked how I feel recently and the words that came to my mind were “a weird vacuum of malcontent” and “melodramatic”.

I feel like there’s a bottomless pit inside of gut and nothing can fill it up. So I devour everything. Food. Sleep. Netflix. People even though I usually can’t stand them. But maybe, just maybe I won’t feel so empty. So I smile. And I laugh. And spiral hopelessly.

All the personal development and self help experts say the key to happiness is gratitude. Finding things to be thankful for. It has always been a weird concept to me because I assumed that to be thankful for something you have to be thankful to someone. Some overarching power of providence. But then I realised, I do have things to be thankful for. I’m thankful for my partner. And knowing that I am capable of realising that even in this moment brings me comfort. Because life is like eating groundnuts. Tasting one bad nut in a handful can often ruin the taste. And we forget just how many good nuts we’ve eaten. And how many more is still left in the bottle.

I want the rest of the bottle so I’ve been taking walks with Yuki for a few days now and it’s helped with the brain fog. I miss running so much. It made everything in the world right again. Nothing is as depressing when your body is physically exhausted. But still, it is strange that one can suddenly and for seemingly no reason lose control of one’s mind and body.

It feels like I am two different people sharing one body. And this other person, this co-tenant of my being is capable of taking total control. I cannot write and I cannot feel. I can only scream through the walls of my head and hope that I can wrestle myself free. To just be me.
