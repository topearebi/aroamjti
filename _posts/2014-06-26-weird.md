In times of war
In time of famine (brawling)
When days are raw
Brutal, hard and harming

When the stomp of marching feet
And the sounds of steel
Slicing through meat
Congeal

All that can be heard
Are the soundless sounds
Of men behead
Falling to the ground

Tis an eerie sight
All of this
Even some of great might
Give this a miss

But the soldiers tho
Would give anything for this
Fighting each foe
With unchastened bliss
