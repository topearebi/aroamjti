I've always wondered
what it would be like to fly
Then one day, I decided to stop wondering
I decided to try (do it)
I took one step, then another
I was on the edge now
I looked down, and I thought
"This is stupid"
But I believe
And if u believe, u can achieve
And that is all that matters in the end; faith
All I need to do is jump
Take a step
So I jumped
It was the most wonderful feeling ever
The wind in my face
The adrenaline coursing through my veins
Heart-rate increasing
I had done it, I thought
I was flying
Then I opened my eye
For some reason, the ground was coming up to me
Would not this earth leave me alone
Was it so determined to bind me forever
Then I realised,
And I could only think;
"WHAT THE...."
Before it was over.....

I stood, staring at my reflection in the mirror
As I had a thousand other times
But this time was different
I didn't recognise this person looking back at me,
Or whom as I was looking it
It was hard to tell the difference sometimes
Its gotten worse these days
I feel like a puppet,
Being manipulated by strings
My actions, not Mine own
And today, in this moment
I couldn't tell,
If either of us was real
We had never looked like this before
Never been this way before
Our hair,
Long, dishevelled
Our wrists slit
Eyes, bloodshot
Mouth, gaping wide open
Clothes, wet
We had failed, though it was only our first time
There would be another
There would be others
I'm not sure which one of us thought of it first
Which one of us came up with the idea
Which one of us really wants this
I'm not sure if I am the reflection,
Or the person
Or both
I'm not sure of anything
Is this my goal
Or is it her's
Why she wants this tho,
I know not
All I know is,
I have committed to it
And I will commit it
SUICIDE, that is
