I've never liked filters. For the same reason I've never been truly able to appreciate make-up and body modification surgery. This is not because I have a purist ideafication of the human body. I do not believe that we are what we are and we should not interfere with God's plan or anything so contrived. In fact, I believe quite the opposite. That the condition of being human is to constantly be evolving. Changing. Adapting.

But it is one thing to evolve, another entirely to pretend.

I mention this because despite this clearly being a filtered picture and no exactly what I look like, people seem taken by a picture in which the lighting is obviously digitally manipulated. While I definitely do not begrudge the idea, I find it intriguing.

The first little paragraph of this post has been idling in my drafts for what is probably a few weeks now. Part of the reason for that I suppose is due to being engaged on developing other creative outlets for expressing my angst. Another part I suspect is also due to a lack of accountability. I owe it to no one to publish anything, and so I don't.

When I put it that way however, it opens my mind up to the possibility that I do not value myself very much, at least in comparison to the value I place in other people if I think that accountability to myself does not mean much.

What is interesting though is that sequel to that paragraph at the beginning, I am in the process of somewhat evolving. I am experimenting with filters. Just like cutting videos to produce a certain effects, filters can provide a certain tone to the story that a picture is trying to tell. They can create a mood, which is the way I believe they should be used. Not to give yourself tinier eyes or fuller lips but who am I to judge how people choose to view themselves through the eyes of a lens.
