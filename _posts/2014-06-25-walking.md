Walking into the open door
Walking across the narrow hallway
Walking on to the end of the path, my path
Walking onward
Walking through the debris falling all around me
Walking along as the structures around me collapsed
Walking forward as the very foundations under my feet shook
Walking unshaken as the very earth beneath me gave way
Walking towards the end of the path, my path
Walking away from the light, to thee darkness
Walking from light
Walking to the arms of death
