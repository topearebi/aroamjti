date: 2023-09-22 17:03:00 +0000

You are on my mind when I wake up. 
And when I fall asleep. 
Every second that I am not with you,
I spend wanting you. 
Hoping for you. 
Thinking about how I could be making you smile
I love it when you smile.

I think of what your voice sounds like in the morning
And I listen to it
I think of what your face looks like 
And I stare at it
I can probably describe your face from memory. 
I have studied you so much that I remember you without seeing you.

Just like I need my cup of tea in the morning. 
I need you every day.
