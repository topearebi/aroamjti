"What do you want from me?" she asked
"Nothing" I said.  "I just want you"
"Why me?" She asked. "I'm not worth it" she said
"Yes you are" I said. "I love you"
"You're just like every guy" she said. "You're only after sex" she said, with tears in her eyes
And so I turned around, and walked away. Knowing I had been caught
