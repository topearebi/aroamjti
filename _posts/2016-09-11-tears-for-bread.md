Tears for bread

                              Death
                              Sadness
                              Despair
                              Pain
                              Hunger
Lost. So lost                 Hate
Very lost                     Hunger
                              Pain
                              Hunger
                              Hunger
                              Hunger

I'm too sad to be alive


            Life is not a rainbow
            It cares not what color you feel
            Nor how much rain you've seen

My coat of many colors
Got lost in the rain

    I'm too happy to die

                              Drown me
                              Kiss me awake

              find me

                              Tears on my pillow head

    I'm too hurt to cry
