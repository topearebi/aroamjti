His hand guided as a father
His invisible hand like no other
Where he leads you do not know
But where he leads you will still go
You believed he would never lead you astray
For he had declared "I will never lead you the wrong way"
And you did his words believe
Why you did, you know not now, but you thought him unable to decieve
Now he has abandoned you
In this dark place, with nowhere to run to
Alas he did lead you astray
His hand did guide you, but it guided you the wrong way
Of where you are now you have no doubt
A place where you can all you want, but no-one will hear you shout
He has guided you, and guided you well
He has lead you all the way to your own private hell
But of laying blame on this father, you should not make the mistake
For it was you, who did his false hand take
