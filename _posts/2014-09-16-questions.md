I swear I have the wrong friends. Where are the writers? The readers. The thinkers. Philosophers. Why do they not gather around me? Why do we not flock together? Are we not the same? Of similar mind. Similar goals. Then why do I walk this world alone? Surrounded by strangers I call home. Where are the people of my ilk? My kin. Why are we not drawn together? Or perhaps, are we not so similar after? Am I lacking? Maybe I'm not who I think I am. Maybe I attempt only to escape my fate. To escape being plain. Ordinary. Normal. I attempt to claw myself out of this cage of limited humanity. I want to feel what its like to not feel. Feel what others do not. Discard what others cherish. Maybe my kind do not flock around me because I am not one of them. Not by birth. It does not come naturally to me. Difference, indifference. I am different from the different. That makes me normal. But even then, should I not be among my kind? The pretenders. Wannabes. But still, no, I am not thrust amidst those I am. Nor those I want to be. I walk among originals. Original in their own way. Not in a way I accept, but original nonetheless. And among them, I am a fake. A fraud. I am alone. Though they contend with me for now. I wonder how long it will take them before they see it. I hope they do not. I desperately wish they do not. Because no matter how hard I complain. How silently I cry. I still want to be with them. For now. I want to be somewhere. Even if I don't like it. Even if I'm alone. As long as there are with me. I can bear the loneliness. No matter how much I tell myself otherwise. I am desperate to be accepted. I just want to be normal. But I don't want to be normal. Maybe that's why I will never be. Maybe herein lies the key. Maybe I'm the only one of my kind. Stuck inbetween. Who else could as indecisive. As unsure. This must be it. Why I'm alone. Because there's only me. There was only me to begin with. There's none like me. Maybe I don't need them - The ones I want to be -. They are the ones who are really alone. Alone together. For they are all the same. Does it not then defeat my purpose. To be different. I give up. There's no point in trying to make sense of it.
