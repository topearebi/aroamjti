I love you like adrenaline through my bones
And I'll admit that, your kiss
Is a little like heroin running through my veins
But you are a reminder of a time when
Survival was the only thing I knew
You are a fix I've never needed to be whole
See I was never broken,
Just under construction
And I promise to never forget,
The pieces of you that built me up
Into the woman I was all along
