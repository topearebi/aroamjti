Laziness is inactivity resulting from a dislike of work
Laziness is apathy and inactivity in the practice of virtue
Laziness is the nylon sitting in the corner of my room for the last 3 weeks
Sometimes it stirs from it's own laziness,
from just sitting there
When I turn the fan on it goes around in circles,
As though rejoicing that I have decided to let it spend yet another day here
Always in the same spot, as though trying to stay out of my way
Maybe it thinks I don't notice it but I do
Sometimes I watch it at night.
Alone,
Sober,
Content.
I contemplate throwing it away.
The dustbin is barely 2 feet away from it
But it's about 4 feet away from me,
And I want to sleep
Or eat
Or read
Or Something
So I look away
And grant it yet another day
