Ever have those moments where you just can’t stop?
You’ve just gotta write with all you’ve got?
And it’s not even poetry to you anymore
You’re writing to save your life but no one will ever understand why you’re always awake at 2 am when they wake up to pee and 4 am when they wake up to pee again and you’re asleep all day in all your classes you can’t think can’t possibly keep all those words and all that talk in your little head that barely has space for itself and so you get straight Bs and Ds

-and I didn’t even end with what I started with sometimes I don’t know what I’m writing // A.A
