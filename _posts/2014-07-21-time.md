Time conquers all
And time flies
So time destroy quickly
And unhindered
Time is a beast
Savage in nature
Unbiased and uncompassionate
Whether big or small or beautiful or ugly
Time ravages all in its path
And ALL is in its path
But can even time destroy this?
This "thing" we have between us
That connects us, and pulls us together
This thing that evokes feeling in me I've never felt before
This thing that turns me into a passionate beast
Completely at your mercy
Surely this thing is greater than time
Surely, it will outlast even time
