I watched my Rebecca slowly, carefully, as she ate. Making sure to burn every detail of her into my brain, after all, today...no, tonight might be the last time I'll ever see her.

Rebecca, Robbie he called her dressed in full black attire - Boy jeans and a shirt with a promotional picture of some band he had no idea of - was completely oblivious to her father's musings. He had been acting a bit weird all day. Well, he was always weird, but today, just today, he was a bit...off. She sighed audibly. "I'm not letting him ruin today" she thought and inwardly rolled her eyes as she thought of the man's many...eccentricities. If only she'd known

"Robbie" he said so hoarsely that the girl opposite him at the dining table could scarce hear him. Carl cleared his throat, as he always did whenever he had something important to say. As if he could cough out the uncertainty that threatened to choke him. "She's old enough. She should know. Everything will be alright" he said, more to convince himself than anything else. "I'm her father..." "I'M HER FATHER" he thought with growing conviction. Properly psyched up now, he tried again, "Robbie", he said in a voice almost free of strain. "There's something I need to tell you"

Rebecca turned her head up slightly at his voice and looked into his eyes. Wondering if she'd finally get to know what had been eating at him so, and if she really wanted to know *fade to black*

*fade from black*

"I AM STILL YOUR FATHER" Carl screamed at the rampaging teen that was currently trying to determine just how many of the kitchen utensils were breakable. The hard way.

"Its really not that big a deal Robbie" Carl said in another attempt to calm his girl

She turned her eyes on him at that and fixed him with her most hateful stare. Carl felt as if he could feel her rage trying to drill into his skull and put him out for good

"Not a big deal?" She said in a deathly calm voice

Then she began to laugh. She laughed so hard that she shed fresh tears to bring back to life the old dying ones. "You're right. Its not big deal." She said in between laughs. "No big deal. No big deal. No big deal" Rebecca was mumbling now and her eyes had gotten this crazy look to them

Carl had no warning. Well, not enough to save him. In the moment the his Robbie's laughs had turned into that blood curdling shriek and she lunged at him, Carl was already done for. Not even if he had seen the knife could he have mustered up enough courage, or will, to face this...thing that he had created. This was no longer his daughter. He had failed. As a parent, and as a human
