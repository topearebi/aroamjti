title: "Pillow Talk"
date: 2016-10-31 19:00:00 +0000
type: poetry
tags: [heartbreak]
---
I want to talk to you and tell you things.

I want to whisper you secrets as we lay in the dark but my vocal cords lack practice after all these years of disuse.

My throat it tends to collapse under the weight of all the words stuck there.

They form a lump there.

I move up against your form and put my mouth to your ear hoping to put the least amount of strain on my cords 

But all that makes it out is a sob. 

I whimper and I cough.

And again tonight these are the only sounds that I've got.

Sounds that make you look at me in disgust.

And You're a doctor but you don't have the pills to numb this ache I've got

And you're a doctor but you can't cut out this cancer that forms with my every thought.
