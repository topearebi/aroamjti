You know that feeling where you want to do nothing but you can’t stand nothing so you think of so many things but you can’t form a single thought till it feels like you’re about to explode only you can’t and your skin is so confining and maybe you haven’t had your bath too so it stinks and you just want to get the hell out of it and just plain get away but you don’t really want to go anywhere and you know it probably wouldn’t solve your problem anyways so you can’t do anything anymore but just be and go through motions you remember yourself doing sometime when you were probably ordinary and probably didn’t feel so much like such a fucking…thing

And then maybe you start to eat a lot and you start to feel worse cause your stomach is so full and hot and yet it feels like there’s this hole in it you desperately need to fill and what else to fill it with but food and it feels good during but after every plate you feel the whole widen a bit more and your stomach gets hotter and you start to imagine if it will continue to grow till you’re devoured by the thing in you that devours

But you have hope even though it’s such a useless word right not but tomorrow is another day and maybe just maybe you won’t feel so empty no matter how much you stuff yourself and maybe the stress of being so depressed anytime you’re alone will burn off all the calories you ate but you’re not fat anyways your arms and legs are sticks yet every time you stand sideways in front of the mirror you can see that little bump and you can’t deceive yourself that you’re pregnant because you can’t possibly be and it wouldn’t make you feel better anyways even if you could it’d only make you feel worse and you’re ugly and your face is oily the sweat sticks to your skin and there’s no hanky so you wipe yourself with the back of your hand and you feel even more disgusting and it makes you want to cut off your arm.

You’re alone with yourself and you know you’re depressed and depressed people never make good company everyone knows this it’s probably why you’re alone in the first place everyone can smell it off you that you’re sick and you wish that if only you were half as lucky as they are to be able to run away from this thing that has become you and you know it’s not you because you have access to what goes on in your own head and everything is so beautiful there and you wonder how somewhere in between the thoughts in your head got corrupted to form this ugly outer shell…

Or am I the only one who experiences these thoughts
