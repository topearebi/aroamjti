title: "Stuck"
date: 2014-09-04 23:10:00 +0000

You left and it felt like you split my stomach open and watched the blood rush out
My stomach literally fell to my feet
I watched you as you walked away
Too afraid to look at the empty shell I had become in the aftermath of your betrayal
The last droplets of my blood splash against the pavement where you left me long before the sound of your footsteps fade away
My vision sways even as I’m rooted firmly to the spot. Shock, my crutch
My body goes into rigor mortis, my mind being fully convinced it’s dead
I have only a few seconds left to process these thoughts before I’m forever stuck
Somewhere among the mess of thoughts that is now my head, the constant din of the words you said and that I didn’t comes to the fore
I have even less time to wonder if there was anything I could have done to make you stay
And now I’m stuck. Like the words in my throat when I first laid eyes on you and could only write you
Is it mere coincidence that you made me feel the same both at the beginning and the end
