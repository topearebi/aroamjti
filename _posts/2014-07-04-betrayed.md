You gently took my hand
And led me to the flames
You watched me burn
With a smile on your face

I watched you watch me
And felt betrayed
With my heart so heavy
My body could not run away

As the flames licked at my skin
I scarce felt pain
While my skin turned to ash
My heart had long decayed

While in my final moments
The embers sparked rays
Memories of a time
When we both used to play
