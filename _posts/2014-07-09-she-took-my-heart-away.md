My heart has left with her. She carried it in her breast
I thought I'd kept it locked tightly within my meat
But when I tried to feel my chest
It was empty, absent of beat or heat

And now she's gone and far away
I wonder how long I can survive without my heart
To live long enough to still one day
Gaze upon the one who took away my breath
