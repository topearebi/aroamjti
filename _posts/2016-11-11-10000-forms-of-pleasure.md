The gods have given me a gift
And while it must be worth it,
I simply think it too sad,
To crumple up such a beautiful wrapper
Like it was a mere sundress

Your personality gives me pleasure
Even though my fingers sometimes
Seem to lose themselves in the hem of your skirt

- there are 10000 forms of pleasure
  and one of them is waiting to touch you
