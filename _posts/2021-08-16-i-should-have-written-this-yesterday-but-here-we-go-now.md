I am starting a personal challenge to myself to write every day or at least as much as possible for the foreseeable future. I could do one of those 30 or 60-day writing challenges but this isn't a vanity metric. This is an actual attempt to be better and more consistent than I have ever been in my life.

Perhaps it is, unreasonable, to expect that the mere repetition of a certain action can cause a profound change in the nature of a person. But it is definitely true that for years the one consistent piece of advice that has been touted as the linchpin to the success or rather, the realisation of any endeavour is basically to "do the thing". Do the thing as much and as often as possible.

This is me, doing the thing.

Yesterday, this was going to be a post about the gym, something else I'm also attempting to be consistent with. I am unwilling to part with it so I'm just going to add it as a little footnote here regardless.

So there I was, roughly 4-5 sets left to completing my workout and being lazy about it as I should. I look outside the window and what do I see? Someone had parked a bloody Camaro outside. And was inside, working out with the rest of us like it was normal.

I mean it's bloody bumblebee.


Something else I found interesting was this; you know how there's the unspoken and sometimes even spoken rule that you do not go to the gym to pick up people you're potentially attracted to? I mean, people do not come to the gym for matchmaking purposes.

Well, they do, but they also don't.

There was this girl who was arguably the most attractive person in the gym at the time due to reasons (body and skin colour). She seemed to be getting a fair amount of attention but she seemed generally unfazed. I overhear one conversation where some dude is telling her something about "you didn't know who I am" sounding very cocksure of himself. It was admirable in a way and yet annoying in the way cocksure people are annoying.

Fast forward to a few minutes later and who do I see leaving the gym but this same person, and she's waiting just outside the door for some reason.

As it turns out, cocksure guy is Bumblebee's driver. And right there and there, he pulls a Shia LaBeouf in Transformers 1 act because it turns out, the girl from earlier is his Megan Fox and they leave together.

I suppose the lesson here is, get a Transformer. I wonder if Optimus Prime would have the same effect, rolling up to the gym in a truck.
