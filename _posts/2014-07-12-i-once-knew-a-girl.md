I once knew a girl
For this girl, hard I fell
But she left me, quick as a toll of a bell
Faster than my eyes could tell
Her leaving, leaving me in an on-earth hell
But still, for this girl, my soul I'd sell
This girl, that weighs on my heart like a dumbbell
what I wouldn't give, for one more twirl
To once again hold her in my arms and smell
The aroma of the devil, the scent of a girl
