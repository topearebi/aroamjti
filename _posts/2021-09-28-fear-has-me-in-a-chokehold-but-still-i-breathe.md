The first time I felt true fear was two days ago as I was making a right turn just before jubilee bridge. The road is terrible so I had to drive real slow. And in between the moments between where I was carefully massaging the break pedals my heart suddenly constricted as I, for the first time, fully contemplated the consequences of my own mortality; If I died, right then, in that moment, I would have left nothing behind except people who will slowly learn to forget me. Worse, I will have no idea how the person I love the most in this world is this doing. It is this not knowing that had stuck it’s fist in my chest.

I have since made peace with the fact but I nonetheless remain aware of the fact that death makes us powerless. It strips us of all agency except that which we can exert through memory. The things we do that make such an impact the dust only settles long after we have passed on. It is possible, and maybe even necessary to live so fully and so we’ll that we matter across time. That the halo of our existence continues to shine in the space that will result from our absence.

Then maybe we will continue to be useful to the people we love. Maybe they will continue to love us
