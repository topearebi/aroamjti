And so I stood
Alone. Lost, and hopeless
On the brink of despair
On the edge of insanity
I took one last look back
To the world I was leaving behind
It was so empty. Cold
When I last saw it
I thought I had let go of everything
Everything binding me to that place...this place
But this time, that I looked back
It was different
Was it me, or had something changed in the world I had just turned my back on
There was a light there this time
A light that shone through all the darkness and despair in my eyes. In my world
And it touched me
And I felt, knew, I had a reason to live
And everything would be better
As long as I had this light
And that light, is you
