Today we had our first fight.
Though it was brief and limited to daylight
It marked the beginning of our end not yet in sight
Broken voices were all we had to show for it by night

We fought again upon this day
The reason for it I forget till today
Both of us wanted to hurt not play
Broken furniture we had to show for it at the end of the day

Broken plates and cracked cups
Cracked shutters and broken countertops
The more we grow the more frequent the love stops
And each time it stops its level drops

Broken bones; the new orgasm to our rumps
This time, we may have gone a little too far
And for why? You were once my star
But now you stand my wrath's harbinger

I'm sorry but I can't do this anymore
Not with all that's gone on before
I'm not gonna stick around till you walk out the door
Show me one thing we haven't broken before
I love and cherish you, my wife no more
I'll drop my broken ring on the floor
And leave you to pick up the pieces of our broken tour
