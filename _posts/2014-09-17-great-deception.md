We all take life seriously
Considering our options
Thinking things through
Calculating all possibilities
We are scared of making the wrong choice
But if religion is anything to go by
And destiny is to be believed
We are the greatest fools
Fooling ourselves
Deluding ourselves into believing that we are "playing it safe"
We say we have no choice
We say everything is written and known by a "GOD"
Then we try to make choices
Telling ourselves we shape our destiny
Telling ourselves we have free will
Going against our very own concept of God
The Creators Of Our Own GREAT DECEPTION
