Once upon a time, I wanted to be a model. I had this idea that people would call me up and pay me to wear clothes and take pictures that make me look good. So of course I asked people who worked with models what they thought of my chances. I did not become a model.

Earlier in the month while launching Cowreeh, we needed to do a product shoot. And not just any product shoot. At Cowreeh, we are not just building a store or any regular old fashion label. We are building a lifestyle brand. A collective of people that believe that understand that there is no limit to the ways in which a person can look good and feel good. So we needed to look the part.

Now where do you get models that can portray your brand image on short notice A photographer and director that understand it? We had and have continued invest a lot in terms of time and money on building every inch of this brand from scratch. If that experience and Thanos have taught us anything, it's that sometimes the answer is simply "Fine, I'll do it myself". I became a model.

In life, we will want many things we will fail to get a lot of them. We will even be denied them. People will reject us. Life will reject us. Despite our best or worst efforts.

It is a popular saying that the patient dog gets the fattest bone. It is also a popular derision of the saying, I imagine, that the patient dog will get nothing at all. Patience is oft considered a virtue. A patient man or woman is considered the ideal partner. If you are going to accomplish anything though in a capitalist society however, ambition and being a go-getter would be a more suitable trait to have. Business management is populated with terms like First to Market and Minimum Viable Product because everyone wants to be the first dog. No one ever started a business aiming to be last in line for market share.

Patience can be a deadly virtue to have in the pursuit of accomplishments. You can wait for people to hand you opportunities if and when they think you're deserving, or you can set up your phone camera and take the bloody pictures in the sun.
