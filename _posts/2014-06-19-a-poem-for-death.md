And so I came upon death
Or she came upon me
And I knew that it was my time
For I alone saw her
Stood in d middle of a sea of people she did
Her ugliness arresting attention, as beauty may
An ugly, beautifully so
Terrifying, soothing
Confusing
Beyond comprehension
And so I stood, opposite death
The swarms of people between us now colourless to me
Had slowed
And yet death watched me
They slowly started to loose shape
And yet, death but watched me
And I watched her
In stunned silence, I thought
Till everything else started to rise
Or I started to fall
I couldn't tell the difference anymore
I couldn't tell anything
Except blue
The sky, so blue
Such beauty. So I blinked
And this time, she also stood over me
Pale, ugly, disgusting
Against the beauty of the sky
Yet somehow seemed to fit perfectly
Life and death
Opposing and contrasting
Yet parts of a whole
Together one
This is why immortality cannot exist
For one does not truly live, till he dies
