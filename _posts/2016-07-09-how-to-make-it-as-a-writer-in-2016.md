To sound
Important
You just need
Metaphors
And
Similes
And an overwhelming ability
To use big words

contrast things
Like
"I listen to
The deafening silence
Of
Your dead
heart beating"
And

"Your spirit
animal
Is stegosaurus
With
a pinch of
Avian dna
In its
Deoxyribonucleus"

Post it on
Twitter
Instagram
Anywhere you have
A thousand
Followers
To kiss your arse
With retweets
And likes
Then

Look out
You're a star
Shining so bright
You might hurt
Your very own eyes
But all I see
Is a reflection
Of the choices
That society
Embraces
