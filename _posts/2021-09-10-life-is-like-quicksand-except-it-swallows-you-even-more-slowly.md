Sometimes it feels like the whole experience of living is being stuck in quicksand except it moves so slowly that you barely notice you're sinking. Right around when when you're reviewing new year's resolutions and you realize "fuck, it's been a while year". I suppose that's why people are so obsessed with achievements. They're like sticks you can hold on to that stop you from sinking too fast.

I've been feeling stuck lately, which is weird because in some sense I've been moving. But nothing feels substantial or enough. Or substantial enough. I suppose at the core of it that's the real reason I tend to get into these moods. I fail to find meaning in the act of living. And I loathe the idea of persisting in something I find no meaning in.

What I find interesting though is that while I am struggling with the profundities of my existence as I always have, I do not feel the same encroaching darkness that I am wont to. Rather, I feel an urgent desperation to crawl back to the light, so to speak.

I like the feeling of motion. Of movement. And I'll be damned if I let myself sink any faster than I have to.
