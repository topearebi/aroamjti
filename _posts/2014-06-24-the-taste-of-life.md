As I lay face down at her feet
Coughing up my own blood and drowning in it

I remembered the life I led
The lies I was fed

With the last of my strength I rolled over to stare up at her face
Hoping to with my final stare plant guilt to twist her fate

But all I saw in her eyes was indifference to my strife
It was then I realised that blood is bitter because it is the taste of life
