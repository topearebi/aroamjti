Can we ever truly know what it means?
Can we ever fully grasp this concept?
This oh-so-beautiful thing called love
Is it the same for everyone?
Is there...........no, CAN there be a set definition for an emotion so strong. An emotion unlike any other?
Do human beings from all walks of life. with different backgrounds, identities, experiences and cultures all feel love the same way
Is this feeling so powerful as to unite the minds of such diverse elements into one unified persona?
Or does the disparity among people of the world so affect them that beauty, love and everything else, means different things to them?
And that as a result of the differences among men, happiness for one, is not happiness for all
If this is so, and unity is just an illusion, or a dream. Something unattainable, but ever there, taunting the human race. In a way akin to the proverbial carrot before the horse
Then differences may never be settled. For no man or group wants nor needs the same things as the next
therefore as long as more than one being draws breath, conflict shall ever arise
wars shall ever be fought
battles forever waged
until all is lost
humanity is dead
and the world, shall come to an end
