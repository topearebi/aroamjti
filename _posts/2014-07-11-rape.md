You try to touch her but she shivers
And you're burning up
You draw closer but she pulls back
Yet she doesn't leave
You're spurred on. Turned on
She doesn't resist
She's backed up against the wall now
She's starting to give in
There's fear in her eyes
Doubt in her mind
But she's starting to realize
That she wants this almost as bad as you want her to
You know soon she'll get it. Soon it'll click
She'll part her lips and not to speak
And you'll take the chance to close yours on it
But when its done
And all the intensity is gone
You open your eyes
And notice her drifting away in hers
You'll understand that you've done it again
You've raped her again
