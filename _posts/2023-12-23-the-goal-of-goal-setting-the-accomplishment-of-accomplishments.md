The problem is not so much having goals as it is keeping them top of mind.  Having them always visible or present to guide your actions. It's not like your resolutions or life plan is somehow burned into the back of your eyelids so that every time you take a step or action you can assess how it fits into each line item on your list.

You can write down your goals on a piece of paper, but how long will it last. Before the words fade out; or the paper gets wet; or you loose it. 

You could use an app but then again that's friction. You'd have to leave the app open everytime all the time. Maybe if there was a floating notes app that could be always on your screen to remind you not to spend so much time scrolling through twitter or bingeing Netflix. But there isn't. 

You somehow have to work towards achieving the things you want by somehow reminding yourself of the things you want constantly, all the time by sheer force of will. And it think that's the worst part of wanting things.

The only way to stay on track with growing is to simultaneously be present in the moment and exist in the frame of mind during which you made those goals so that you have the same drive to accomplish them.

Perhaps this is why journalling is often touted as such an important tool. Perhaps it reinforces the things you want so that you don't have to actively remember and it just becomes a part of you. Perhaps.
