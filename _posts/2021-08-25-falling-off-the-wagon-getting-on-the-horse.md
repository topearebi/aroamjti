I should probably explain how I fell off the wagon so soon. I mean I didn't even last a week but here it goes.

My phone literally stopped charging and I am definitely more comfortable typing on my phone. I suppose I could have done with just using a laptop or some other device which I would have done eventually, but then I fell sick. One of those once in a year really terrible bouts with the ailment that we AAs tend to fall prey too. As I write this I am still in recovery. There were definitely times between these events when I could have written, but I didn't. I own up to that.

However, as much as I am tempted to label this a failure and there was a time when I would. I have learnt over time that it is only truly a failure if I give up. I know that sounds like motivational bullshit but it is what it is.

Cowreeh made a sale today. Like made actual money. After a whole week plus of getting only inquiries and likes for all the work we've put in, we finally got a sale. A few months back I would have been on my way to scrapping it. Throwing it in the pile of things I've tried that just didn't work out. Fortunately, that's a lot harder to do when you have a partner.

The other benefits of having a partner as I am coming to find include things such as easing the pressure of funding capital expenditure. That one should be pretty obvious. It's a lot harder to focus on building products and experience when you're constantly worrying about how to fund their creation.

Having a partner also means doubling your immediate network of potential customers. This is especially invaluable if you're a recluse like me with a pretty limited network to begin with. I can't help but wonder the effects of having multiple partners. A hundred maybe? If one person's brings one person, and each of them bring one person! Nevermind. Let's not get carried away.

Depending on what type of partner obviously, having one means a doubling of everything. Double the energy. Double the effort. Double the marketing. Double the ideas. I'm sure there are downsides as well but I imagine we're not big enough to experience them yet.

I have so many ideas for this project and it feels like we come up with new ones every week. I'm worried we need a creative outlet to let off steam and also allow potential customers to weigh in so we don't end up doing making a bunch of stuff that just sounds great in our heads but no one is actually willing to buy.

Whatever the case, as with all things, the experience is the reward. The joy is in the journey. etc. Do I need a special sign out line because this feels like an abrupt ending. I suppose it is what it is.
