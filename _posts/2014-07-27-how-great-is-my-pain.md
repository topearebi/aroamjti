If the moon were my eye
Would it turn red when I cry?
Would my pain cause the moon to die?
Would my pain cause others pain?
Would my pain not be in vain?
Would I be able to bear it, and still stay sane?
Would it cause the end of my world?
Would it make sharp swords seem dulled?
Would it, in a ball, leave me curled?
Would my pain take away my freedom?
Would it restrict my movement and make me dumb?
Would it make me sin and deny me the kingdom?
Is my pain heavier than the ground?
Is it more than a gazillion, plus one pound?
Is it more high pitched than sound?
(Would it make me scream louder than sound?)
Will my pain one day go away?
Will I one day be able to just play?
And just be all happy and gay
Just how great is my pain?
And what would it be like, if I could feel it
If I could feel anything
For It seems greater than any mountain
Or anything else any power could ordain

I still feel it sometimes
Its easier to ignore now
its almost as they say
But no, it doesn't get better with time. Not really
It just becomes bearable
Sometimes
When I'm alone with people. Real people
It let's me play my part in the world
It dulls its ache
Biding its time
Lying in wait
Till I'm alone with myself
Alone with my thoughts
Then it strikes
It sears through me
Cloaked in a thought
Disrupting other thoughts
Till there's nothing but the pain
Lightning through my cells
Yet the only place I feel the pain is in my heart
Cos in the end, the pain is abstract
And I am pathetic and weak
For letting my emotions get the best of me
