We were different in every way
Which is why I admired her
She was of the world
She was loud
The one with all the friends
Attended all the parties
Knew all the boys
Was chased after by all
Everyone loved her
I was of words
Lonely and alone
I was the Reclusive type
I locked myself up in my room and wrote crappy poetry
Pouring what I thought was my soul into pen and scratching it across paper
