There are a two things I absolutely believe it is necessary to master throughout the course of a life.

- The mind, and
- The breath

The mind with it's ability to control the actions of the body

The breath with it's ability to control the condition of the body constitute the entire domain of willfully existence.

I have failed at both.

My heart races when I see you. My mind is chaos at your every expression.

And so I look forward way to be rid of you while keeping you close.
