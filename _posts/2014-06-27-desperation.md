I've tried time and again
To write my thoughts into words
I give them life with my pen
Stamp them immortal on paper
Yet the world does not seem to better understand what's inside my head
And so this final act of desperation
-Born from a need to be understood
I'll blow my brains across this page
In hope that the world may finally read my mind
