Billy Martin was a mean little kid
Everyone hated him, no-one crossed him in the street
Billy didn't care he was fine with it
Everything was just as he wanted it to be
Just then Billy saw a boy across the street
The boy tried to run but Billy soon caught up with him
Billy held the boy up and threatened him
"Gimme all your money little kid or I'll bash your skull in"
The boy, in fright lashed out at Billy
He swung and he clawed till he caught Billy's eye
Then the boy smiled, twisted and crushed the eye
Billy surprised, screamed out in fright
He dropped the kid and ran off into the night
The boy stood up and quietly followed after Billy
He rolled the eyeball in his palm, keeping Billy in sight
And chuckled to himself, amused by his might
Billy still screaming and still blind
Ran along the road winding through traffic
He made it past 1 car, 2, 3 but not the fourth
It was a truck, that crushed him along the floor
Billy's body popped and his other eye rolled out
Rolled out to the feet of the strange little kid
He smirked and spread out his wings
Black wings that camouflaged against the black of the streets
Rising he flew away
The angel of death disappeared having dispensed punishment
Billy's body was turned to mush
Billy Martin was mean but he was still just a little kid
