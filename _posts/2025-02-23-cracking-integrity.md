One of the very first things we do, before we do anything else, is make promises to ourselves. I'll wake up at this time. I'll do this today.

We are careful making promises to others. And careful keeping them. But promises to ourselves are much easier to make. The consequences are less immediate. There is no one to hold you accountable except you.

Making a promise to someone and failing would fill us with embarrassment and shame. If we did it repeatedly, anyone would assume a lack of respect for the individual was the cause. The same goes for when we do this to ourselves. And the consequences I'm afraid, are much more dire.
