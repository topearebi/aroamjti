title: "She Sits Curled Up"
date: 2014-09-29 11:13:00 +0000
type: poetry
tags: [malice, struggle]

She sits curled up amidst the encroaching doom
Her hands pressed against her head like she could compress herself out of existence
You watch her knowing there’s no escape from this room
It almost breaks you that it doesn’t break you enough, that even in this condition you still want to be her god you like this feeling of omnipotence
You can do and undo you’ve done and undone and it is your doing that she’s come undone
You watch as the tears slip from her eyes and you know this should tear you up inside
You should comfort her and some part of you wants to and some part of you does but you can’t bring yourself to touch this thing that she’s become so you send her thoughts instead, images of a gun
You’re not being mean you’re just taking responsibility and trying to finish, sorry fix this and the obvious answer is for her to kill herself our society’s answer to pain anyways is suicide
