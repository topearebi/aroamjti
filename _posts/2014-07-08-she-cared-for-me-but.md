She cared for me
But she did not love me
She was still in love with him
And her heart was capable of loving only one
They hadn't kissed
But I could smell him on her breath
Her every word, filled with traces of him
And her thoughts, were mostly of him
He was far away, but he stood still
In front of her heart
His back turned to d rest of the world, hiding his face from me
But yet, guarding her heart from me
Shield on his back, lance in his hand
He stabbed constantly at her heart
Making sure she'd never forget him
What was I to do
What I am to do now
To break through this shield
I thought waiting, was the answer
Wait for time to destroy all that would stand in my way
Wait for his shield to crumble from rust
And his lance, break from wear
But time, neutral as it is
Destroys me also
And my heart too does wear on, and tear
Crumbling slowly, piece by piece
Falling off, to be lost forever
What would happen, when my heart is all gone?
I would rather not find out
And so I will fight
And if I cannot have her heart
I will have her body
And press upon it
Till I break through skin
Write my poetry on her flesh
Till she is lost in my words
And face the enemy
And I will strike him down
For hurting the one I love
And keeping our hearts apart
