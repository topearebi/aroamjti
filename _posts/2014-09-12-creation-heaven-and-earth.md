Let me tell you this story. I'm going to make it up as I go. Leave common sense out of it

Once upon a time, there were 2 lovers. Heaven and earth. They were destined to be together. It was obvious. Their love was so strong that they were always together. Everytime. And that's why at the beginning of the world, there was no life. Because there was no space between them for anything to grow. That is how tight they held onto each other

So one day, while heaven and earth were together, a very powerful being came. You may refer him as a god or good. I shall take no such stance however. For he did a very cruel thing. He looked upon the land. And saw the darkness that it was. He saw that the cause of the darkness was heaven and earth's love for each other. They were so tight together, there was no space (get it? Space :D)

He saw the world as he wanted it to be. As it should be. And thought nothing of the beauty of the love between heaven and earth. And he ordered them to separate and create space. For he had a vision of how the world should be, and it was good.

Heaven and earth of course refused. The god grew angry at being disobeyed. He knew he was right. He wanted to create. Why couldn't they understand

So he tore them apart. And threw heaven up up high and cast earth to the ground so far, that they would never be able to meet no matter how much they stretched

Earth, the woman, was filled with so much longing for heaven that her heart literally pulled at him. So she could hold her lover once more. But all to no avail. No matter how hard she pulled, heaven could not come down. The god had so designed it that it would be impossible for them to meet again. But still she pulls to this very day. And this is what is now known as gravity

When they realized it, for they could speak to one another. Something they had never needed to do b4. They were terrible at it. Unable to gauge each others volume. She spoke with the wind. And he with thunder. (And thunder is loud today because heaven does not realise that he is shouting)

Sha sha(this is where I lost track), when they realized this, heaven was soo stricken with grief, that he shed tears. Yes, tears. He cried. Cried for his love, and his love. The one he would never get to be with again. He who had never known pain, had never cried, shed tears. And they fell and fell. For 40 days. Till the world was flooded. When his tears dried up, and his eyes cleared. He looked upon his beloved and panicked when he saw what he had wrought. Earth was nowhere in sight. She was covered under a layer of water. And only the fear of causing further harm stopped him from crying any further

After a few days tho the water cleared (sucked down by gravity or something) and earth spoke to heaven. And spoke of how happy she was. Heaven was confused

But she told him, that tru it all, they had finally found a way to be togeda. Even if it was only a part

Till today, heaven and earth are bound together only by the rain

And trees never reach the sky cos men love more than women

*shrugs*
