Darkness dawned upon the land
As it had dawned upon my heart
The raven, heralding the coming of the night, and the fall of the light, cawed in the darkening sky
I looked upon where the light once stood
The sun, in its glory and oppressive heat
Now removed from the sky
A great giant, shunned by the earth
Which forever continues to turn away from it
The beauty of the darkness
Which brings blindness
And a coldness to the body of others
And to this heart of mine
This heart, an embodiment of the night
This heart, cold and black
This heart, which pumps no blood
This heart which pumps no more
But rather, has assumed a deathly stillness to it
Calm, and at peace
A dead heart
In a living body
Devoid of feeling
A body, that has a puppet become
A puppet, with no puppeteer
A puppet pulling its own strings
A puppet, its own master
A puppet with no desire to be real
A puppet devoid of contentment, content with being incontent
A puppet lost
A puppet confused
A puppet with a heart, as useful as one with none
A puppet with no inclination whatsoever, to find OZ
A human puppet
A living puppet
A human dead
Alive in skin only
Of a dead heart, and a dead mind
Of rotting corpse, not visible
But an inner rot
A corroded brain
A feast for maggots
A mind which thinks no more
A mind inept
A mind blank
A mind white
A mind, plain as a beginning, at its end
A mind, with no minding
A mind unminded
Of a blank soul
Devoid of memories
Devoid of humanity
Devoid of animality
A soul interred
An immortal soul
Dying
A soul with no soul
A sorrowful soul
A lonesome soul
A soul, to be pure, but corrupt in its emptiness
An impossible soul
A complex soul
Stripped to its bare, its simplest
An un-understandable simplicity
A simple complex
A complex, simply empty
An empty soul
A vessel soul
Filled with nothing
And so nought can enter
An incomplete soul, finished and at its end
A DEAD soul
