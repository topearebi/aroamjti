Dish washing requires optimising for so many different things that it's no surprise people hate it. 

You have to make sure you are

1. Using as little water as possible
2. Using as little soap as possible
3. Keeping the sponge a clean as possible
4. Pouring as little oil and food down the drain as possible

If all these things do not bother you while you're doing the dishes, you must she some sort of sociopath.
