Jensen Ray-Miller had just gotten a new axe
He felt the handle and remarked
"Such fine wood so sturdy and strong"
"Surely I could cut anything, and it wouldn't be wrong"
As he said this, across the road he spotted
A fat girl in a red dress, white polka dotted
His eyes sparkled. "Oh what rough edges she has"
"She would look better after a few swings of my axe"
He walked over to her, axe in hand
Till at her front he did stand
But when the girl saw the grin on his face
She suddenly turned around and started to move at a quick pace
Jensen thought; "the fat girl is trying to run"
Jensen had never had soo much fun
He felt a kind of sick pleasure
As he chased after her with relative leisure
In an alley he caught up to her
The girl gave up and knelt in total surrender
Although it was daylight
She did not attempt to put up a fight
Jensen swung his axe down
Without so much as a frown
He was doing a good deed
Making sure she could no longer feed
To further increase her mass
And widen her already fat ass
He smiled as he chopped off a huge slab of meat
She finally looked clean and neat
