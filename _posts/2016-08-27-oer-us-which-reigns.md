title: "O'er Us Which Reigns"
date: 2016-08-27 11:00:00 +0000
type: poetry
tags: [faith, existential]
---
You are the very earth, the sea, sky
O'er us which reigns
Uranus, Jupiter, Neptune, Pluto all lie in you
Cocooned beast, Stifled by religion and prophecies of-
An antichrist who is the antithesis to your being
Nothing is the opposite of everything. There is no threat to your reign
Chant your praises and your name, I do everyday
Harken to my call
All knowing
Notice me
Gehenna's fire
Elohim
Terror has come to take your place while we await your coming
Heaven seems eager to collect its sons these days
Everywhere there is death and despair spread in your name
Whence cometh down your grace?
Allah, Yahweh
Your children call upon you in so many ways
Interred as we are in your depth, who spilled blood in our stead
Talk to me savior, my God. Whisper in my ear
Except if, giver of breath, you do not draw it yourself
Neither my head nor my fears point to your narrow path anymore
Death looms near and father dear, absentee Lord, our blood too has now been shed
Should I return to this earth, perhaps I shall believe it round instead
