And so I stood against my opponent
A face-off
Between brother and brother
Once lovers now enemies of the vilest nature
My brother took a step forward, then another
Advancing to attack, he charged
Yet, I moved not an inch
I did not move to defend, nor to attack
My brother should have been wary
But he believed too much in his own strength
Too much in himself
With good reason too
For he had won every other time we faced-off
But this time would be different
This was my chance for victory
And I would seize it. Firmly
And my brother fell
Right before my feet
His fingers grasping for me as he fell to the bottom of the earth
For he had not noticed the hole I dug right before my feet
He did not notice, because I goaded him on
With my poseless taunt
Which fired his pride, and drowned his life
