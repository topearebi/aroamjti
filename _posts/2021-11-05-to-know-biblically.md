You can never really know someone. Really know someone. You can know things about them. Like the colour of their hair, their favourite band, what they smell like after two days without a bath. But these are merely outward manifestations of a more complex condition. The fever is not the malaria. Nor is the headache. But we must treat these things for a cure.

Like medicine, the best we can hope for is patterns. To discover them. To recognise them. Because it is these patterns. These collections of things we know about a person can shed some insight into some small aspect of who a person really is.

So you take their favourite band, and whether or not they like Ice Cream, and you put that together with whether or not they like to cuddle after sex and we tell ourselves that we know who they are. Maybe we even know what they want. But we do not. Not really.

The best we can do is identify patterns and place them against what we know and see what fits best. But we never really see the whole picture. We do not see that they lay awake all night watching us sleep. We do not hear the little promises they are constantly making to themselves every day about us, about their cat. We do not know what makes them tick.

For most people the closest we will ever come to knowing someone at their core is about 6 inches from the pelvis. That is not to say that sex is not a very intimate act. It really is. But you can have amazing sex in under 30 seconds in a parking lot with someone you just met 30 minutes ago and it will mean absolutely nothing in the next 30 hours. You can have sex with the same person for a decade, or two and end it with a messy divorce that leaves you regretting the first half of your life. So much that you consider the first half of your life.

Surely if there is some meaning to gleaned here it is that sex is not magical beyond the moment. And that no matter how close you get to someone, with mixed limbs and saliva and genitalia you, can never really know them. Perhaps it is because we get so close, that we can no longer see the patterns. Just their favourite ice cream flavour and how much we love it that they let us take a bite.
