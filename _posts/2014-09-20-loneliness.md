As I stood over that which I once had loved
My flames ever consuming that which I once cherished
The dark tears I shed unable to quell what I had wrought
Heart and mind, separate and apart
No longer united in cause or action
Parts of a whole, now parts of none
For I was also broken
A part of me that I never knew existed
That never DID exist before now
A part of a part
Separate of mind and heart, body and soul
A part of me
That is nothing
It was hollow
it ate at me
And devoured everything in me
Everything I am
Till I was cold and empty inside
This is what I had searched for
This is what I wanted
This is why I destroyed all
For this feeling
This LONELINESS
