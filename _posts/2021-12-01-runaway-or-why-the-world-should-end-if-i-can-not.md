Sometimes I wish you were dead. Or that I was dead. Or that the whole world would end. Had ended.

Sometimes I wish that I had never been born. That I did not have to live so long. And I know that it has only been twenty-something years but it has been oh so long. Twenty-something years too long.

I am not a pessimist I do not think that the world is so terrible that I need to escape from it. I do not think that the world is a terrible place. I do not think that you are terrible or that people are terrible. I mean they are terrible but not enough that I would want to permanently avoid them. No, if it had to do with people or the nature of the world I could simply take a break. I would simply take a break. Like I always do I am very good at running. A few years ago I would run for two hours every day and it was my first run-in with actually being consistent at something and being good at something. Though I could not tell you, if you asked me, if I was consistent because I was good or good because I was consistent. But you have not asked me. I hope that you will not ask me.

I often considered how possible it would be to run all the way to the other side of the earth. I do not consider how easy it would be but how possible because if it were easy then I could probably never get away from myself. Perhaps if the journey is long enough and hard enough, parts of me will break down from the sheer effort. They will fall off to the wayside so to speak. Be crushed underfoot with the dirt and sweat and all the other frailties that make me so painfully human. So painfully me.

I cannot help but conclude that I am the source of my own disenchantment with life. The architect of my own pain. The warden to my very own prisoner. And yet I am also the prison. My flesh, the confining mechanism to which I am bound. With which I am bound. My singular task, the one at which I have consistently failed for the last twenty-something is merely to be free from me. Free to be me.
