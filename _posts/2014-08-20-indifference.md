They are all the same
They all say they are different
They all say there's none other like them
But that only makes them more like the others
There's nothing different about them
Nothing unique
As long as they are all human,
Their capabilities are all the same
No-one is special
No-one is unique
No-one is different
And so therefore, I shall treat them all the same
With INDIFFERENCE
