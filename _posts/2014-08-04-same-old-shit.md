Life is like a continuous train ride
Continuously going round and round
Always following the same routine
With only a few laughs in-between
At first it excites you
The scenery and landscape entice you
But after a while
It starts to bore you
The scenery and landscape stop to entice you
It no longer excites you
With only a few laughs in-between
Always following the same routine
Continuously going round and round
