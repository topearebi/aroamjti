Its like I'm 5 again. Or 15. Wondering who I am and who I want to be. Who I could be. But I don't know people. What people are like. What they could be. What they have to do to be those things.

So I look for them in stories, I try to live all the lives I could live. Through books, movies, music. Desperately wishing that someone else already found the answers and I could simply copy them.

But no matter how many stories I lose myself in, they never become a part of me.
