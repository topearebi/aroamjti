The eyes are the windows to the soul
If this is so, then it would explain a lot
It would explain why my soul was so dirty
So muddy and tainted
As I open my eyes
So also do I leave open the windows to my soul
Exposing it to the harshness of the world
The visions I see, taint my eyes
And scar my soul
My soul is black
Because I open my eyes
Is this the price to be paid for my curiosity?
For wanting to see the world
Wanting to experience it with all my senses
If only I had known
If I had but been aware
Oh how I envy the blind
Who can no longer see the filth of the world
Whose souls will/can be tarnished no longer
For their windows are forever shut
How I envy those born blind even more so
Who have never had to see
And be disgusted/repulsed by mother nature
And by life
They who are of pure souls
Surely if there is a kingdom after death
It is for the blind
What then must I do?
To secure myself a place in this kingdom
Shall I just close my eyes to the world?
And all of its filth
But if I just close my eyes
Surely temptation must linger
To open my eyes
For man, born from dirt
Shall forever be attracted to it (the "dirty")
What then must I do?
To secure myself a place in that kingdom
IF there is one
And this is a big IF
But I have no hope left in this life
So why not place the remains of my faith in the possibility of another
These are my thoughts
These words you have read with your eyes
Which will now taint your soul too
Are the reasons I gouged my eyes out
