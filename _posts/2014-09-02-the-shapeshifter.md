He is forever without form.
Unpredictable.
Unreliable. He can stand out or blend in.
He has no fear, no feelings.
For he is never the same person.
Fron his birth to his death, ever-changing, he is not responsible for any of his actions.
Because he did not commit them.
There is no "he", no "him".
He has no personality.
He is an "it", and "it's" life, is a hollow empty shell
