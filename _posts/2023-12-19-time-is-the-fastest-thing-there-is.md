All you do is sleep and wake up a few times and all this time is behind you. A whole year has gone by and all you've done is repeat the cycle. Sleep and wake up.

Sure you've had a few wins if you think positively. You've done some things that matter for some people that matter. But ultimately, where you are now and where you want to be might as well be on opposite sides of the planet. All you're doing is adding points on a Cartesian plane one by one. And it goes down to infitiny in four directions.
