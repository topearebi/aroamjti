One day I will lay dead
I could die from not being fed
Or having cut off, my head
But I'd much rather die in bed
Most preferably, the bed in my shed
Yes, my home I do refer to as shed
And I also hope that people will shed
Tears of sadness, anger and dread
Although I'd really hate if they were to break bread
They should at least pretend to be depressed
And act as though, even if a little, they cared
Oh, I also want a coffin made of lead
For no special reason. Yes, I know its weird
Finally, for my epitaph, I want just the letter "zed"
That is how I wish to signify my end
