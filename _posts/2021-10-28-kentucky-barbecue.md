As at 2009, the first Kentucky Fried Chicken outlet opened in Nigeria at 
the Onikan city mall in Lagos. It was also in Lagos, as with seemingly all 
things, that the rumors first began.

It is generally known that Kentucky Fried Chicken sells Fried chicken as is 
implied by their name, but they offer this delicacy in two very different 
ways. One is the regular roasted chicken while the other is the Crispy 
chicken. This divide between the two might seem minute but all parties 
involved know there is a stark difference.

Patrons of the place have been known to walk out without spending a kobo of 
their preferred choice is not available, a decision not easily made among 
Lagosians who dread walking out of shops without empty hands (a condition 
which is currently being studied as a legitimate phobia. It is said that 
the condition will be included in the next revision of the DSM V) and as 
such every outlets makes sure to keep appropriate numbers of both available

Over time however, the quality of their chicken offerings is said to have 
dropped drastically and to be approaching standards similar to those of 
chicken republic. It was then that the rumors started circulating. Rumors 
of a secret third variety of Chicken so spicy and juicy it would remind you 
of one time Barcelos. Some people say on your first bite you could 
literally see the Captain's face morph into the Barcelos cock.

All attempts at investigation into the existence of this so called Barbecue 
chicken have proved futile, but I'm some nights, you might walk in to a KFC 
outlet and notice people, frozen midbite into their chicken. Juice dripping 
down their chin and a look of ecstasy painted on their faces
