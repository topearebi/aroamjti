There are a very limited set of things that I make time for. That I choose to make time for. And I choose you.

In the morning when I wake up, I choose you. I choose to look for your messages. To tell you good morning and ask you how your night was without me. Because as much as I wish that you sleep well everynight, I want all your nights to be with me.

I want to be the last thing on your mind when you go to bed. And the first thing on your mind when you go to sleep.

I choose you when I miss you. And look at your pictures and think of how your face is perfect. How every hair. Every feature is set just in the perfect place. To make the perfect face that I have come to love.

I choose you when I watch you smile. And blush. And when I drop everything else just to see your face for a few seconds and hope that you are just as excited to see me too.

I choose you when you tease me. With your hands, fingers, legs, feet. Every hint of your golden skin. Every day that I get a little piece of you I just want even more and I choice you all of over again.

I choose you.

Love. Always
