title: "Halo of Missing You"
date: 2016-11-30 12:00:00 +0000
type: poetry
tags: [love]
---
I can tell that you're tired but though you say that you look it too
I seem to only see your face shrouded in an halo of 'God I bloody miss you'
I grow tired of staring at pictures
I'd rather the silence of your breathing next to me than a thousand words in your absence
Come home to me that I may once again sleep peacefully
Nothing is the same when you're not right here next to me
Everything is less beautiful that I cannot share with you
