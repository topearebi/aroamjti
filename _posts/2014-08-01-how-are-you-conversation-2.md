How are you?
I'm good. You?
I don't know how I am
I merely exist in space
Moving unmoved
Speaking with mouth shut (talking not speaking)
Listening while tuned out (hearing not listening)
Seeing through sleeping (sleepy) eyes
And I don't know how to feel again
I haven't felt in a long time
I don't remember what its like to feel
I don't remember what its like to be real
I want to be real again,
But I don't want to give up this absence from real life
Because if I am real again, I'll feel pain
Pain comes with reality
And the pain is too real for me
It'll force me back into my shell
To escape from real life
And make me unreal again.
Make me fake again
And the cycle will continue
