I hope you lay awake at night, restless, just thinking of me
I hope when you're asleep, you are plagued with visions of me
Whether good or bad, I just hope you think of me
I hope when you wake, your first thought is of me, and I make u smile
I hope you have pictures of me you keep somewhere that you turn to at random times, just cos u miss me
I hope every time you look at someone's face, the first thing you see is mine
I hope every time you feel down, and a tear comes to your eye, you think of me, know I'm here for you, and you smile
I hope when you're happy, you think of me, cos you want to share your happiness with me, because I'm important to you
I hope when you think of or see random funny or sad things, the first thing you think of is that you want to share the moment with me
I really do hope that you do all this, so I can say I do TOO
