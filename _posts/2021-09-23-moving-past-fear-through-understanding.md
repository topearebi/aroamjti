I have fallen sick again and it has me kind of worried. I know it is malaria and I basically get bitten by mosquitoes everyday but these are strange times. I am getting a blood test.

Going to the hospital is quite a chore because of the distance between where I live and my current insurance coverage so I tend to self-diagnose and self-treat. It usually works because I almost always exclusively come down with malaria, a condition I am certain tons of people can relate too. I have at this point had it so much I can usually recognize the symptoms a few days away.

The most striking thing about this hospital visit, since as I guessed it is malaria, was when the matron who I have known since I was pretty much a child said she still “looks sweet 16”. While she is definitely pretty I found the statement striking because I was at the time considering how so much time has passed since I have been getting treated at the same hospital by the same people and the only evidence of it is our bodies. A metal plate and screw in my knee, recently installed computers after decades of a somehow always functional paper filing system, new waiting chairs and bigger bellies on all of us

I think the reason we all desire to look young is because we know we’re dying. And we are afraid of it. Our skin wrinkling and darkening is akin to jumping off a ledge in slow motion. All true fears stems from a fear of dying.

Scared of heights? No, what truly scared of is going splat on the hard ground. Scared of open spaces, coded spaces? Anything could get you. Scared of strangers? You don’t know what they want and maybe what they want is to kill you.

I understand that this may not happen at surface level for everybody. Most people don’t understand why they are afraid of what they are afraid of and fear can be such an overwhelming emotion that when one feels it, one does not have the capacity to explore or attempt to understand it. And when one is not afraid, the idea of voluntarily revisiting such a jarring emotion may seem more more than just a little unappealing.

Regardless, we must all face our fears. The fear of being sick. Of getting old. Of the old unknown. We are going to die, but probably not right now.

I also saw this hilarious clip of a mosquito trying to bite a person's hand but failing and I wish I had the secret to replicating that.
