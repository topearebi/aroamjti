Remember....how good we were together? How good we looked together? How right it all felt. I completed you and you completed my sentences. We liked the same things, inseparable as like conjoined twins. I was the head, the man but you were the body, my spine, my spinal cord. My feelings for you bypassed thought. I who was always reasoned, reasonable, deep in thought. My eyes lit up my lips split apart at the sound of your voice. My thoughts grew dull. I sputtered, stuttered but you understood. You were thinking the same things as me we didn't need words. We were all that existed to each other our faces shone like headlights in deer eyes. We were deers in headlights. We thought we were safe in our semblance and thus proceeded to lose each other in one other but. We never found ourselves and lost in each other what we sought in one another, someone the same but we are not even ourselves anymore. Now as we are nothing, not even incomplete the love is gone and we've reached our forever more. We complete each other no longer and our arguments are just hot air. I never listen and you never speak. You never care and I wonder how I ever did.

Now we bide our time till one of us snaps and we finally break apart. We're broken anyways maybe we need time alone with our pieces. At least we'll never be alone till we're whole. Then maybe we'll do this again. If I can find you and you've found yourself too. We could play this sick game all over because losing myself in you was the best thing I ever. Falling for you gave me the greatest rush, the depth to your persona made for a longer fall but as with all falls there had to be a landing. Exhilaration turned to crash, wonder to pain and hunger, to something resembling hate.
