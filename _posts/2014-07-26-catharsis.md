I pick up my pen, set it to paper, and prepare to write
But my fingers are shaky as though protesting "not tonight"
By and by I force my will
I bid my trembling fingers stay still
Yet the first stroke I make
Only fairly legible, its snake-
-Like line snakes across the page
As though written by a hand, arthritic with age
Yet I push on, yet I continue to create
Pain on paper, narrating my ghastly fate
With each letter, each word, each line
Each line, each word, each letter departs from fine
As I progress my cathartic attempt
Tears do from my eyes to fall tempt
The emotions in me only swell
As I navigate my way through inflicted hell
I give in to the sin and a tear drop drops
But the one tear heralds them all, and none stops
They soak my paper and the tears cut
They erase my writings, erase my hurt
And once again its just me sitting at my table
To shed anymore tears I am unable
(Finally I'm all empty and alone
Finally I'm home)
