We are so far apart that I can only tell you how much I want you.
Like loving you is a prayer.
I have never been a man of faith yet I worship at the altar of your religion.
Your body is a scripture
The object of my daily devotions

We are so close together that I can tell you how much I want you
Like loving you is gospel
There is no time or space where I did not want you
There is no time or space that I ever stop
