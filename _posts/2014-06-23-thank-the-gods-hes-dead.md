One day I will lay, dead
Then my "friends" will their false skin shed
They'll speak the truth about me
What they really thought of my tomfoolery

They'll open their mouths and shout
About how they hated my mouth
Hated the things it said
They'll thank the gods its with me, dead

Some will talk of my hands
Hands that have never tilled any lands
They'll complain about how; by it they were never fed
They'll thank the gods its with me, dead

Others will speak of my feet
They'll envy how it never stank, even in extreme heat
They'll hate it for always being above each one's head
They'll thank the gods its with me, dead

My wife will talk of my myopia
How I always needed my glasses to see her
She always thought it a bit weird
She'll thank the gods its with me, dead

My kids will speak of me
How they thought I was stingy
They used to beg me till they (their eyes) were red
They'll thank the gods that I am dead
