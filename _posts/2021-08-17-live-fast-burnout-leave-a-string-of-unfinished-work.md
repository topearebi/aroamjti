title: Live fast, Burnout, Leave a String of Unfinished Work
date: 2021-08-17 23:00:00 +0000
---
Now that I'm working I'm something again, I am starting to understand why I dislike working. When I find something I am passionate about, I tend to hyperfocus on it, leaving little space for anything else. I hate distractions. I seem to believe for some as of yet undiscovered reason that the only way to have something you want is to first suffer for it. Go through some sort of trial by fire. And even just reading this I can see how this is problematic.

Things don't necessarily have to be hard to be enjoyed. You don't have to suffer to be deserving. Or at least I hope that is not the case. Or perhaps I put in an average amount of energy and I'm simply overestimating myself. Regardless, there is an obvious relationship between overstretching yourself to reach your goals and the unwillingness to try again having failed a few times.

I suppose this is what is meant by burnout.

I am going to start learning to take things slow. To more enjoy the process of creation and ideation so I don't suddenly become exhausted halfway through.
