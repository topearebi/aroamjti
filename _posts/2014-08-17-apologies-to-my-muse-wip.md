Apologies to my muse
I've blown a fuse
I've lost inspiration
Drowned myself in frustration
But before you make a big fuss
My reason for allowing this are thus;
The fire burned too bright and too fast for starters
Now all that's left are dim embers
Apologies to my muse
I've blown my fuse
My brain's shorted out
The words no longer flow from my mouth
Apologies to my muse
You are no longer of use
You no longer bring me inspiration
Now its just sadness and frustration
Apologies to my muse
I don't mean to be obtuse
But you're all used up
Anything I write about you would flop
