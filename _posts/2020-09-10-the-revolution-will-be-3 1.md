She does not listen when I,
tell her
I look
Absolutely hideous,
In that photograph.

She tells me I,
look gorgeous
I just need to smile
More.

I am a tall,
Glass of hot,
chocolate she says.
And she is
Thirsty with a,
Sweet tooth.

Still,
The picture is,
Absolutely hideous.
Pictures are not
For drinking anyways.
