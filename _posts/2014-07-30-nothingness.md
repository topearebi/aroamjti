You're lonely and it's cold
But there's no way home but your own
This one you made
In your violence with your rage
It's how you express our pain and your hate
But this time it's cost you great
So don't try to fight it don't be defiant
Hunch your back and slouch your shoulders
Drag your feet as you slowly grow colder
Because deep down you know you deserve
Nothing less than this nothingness
