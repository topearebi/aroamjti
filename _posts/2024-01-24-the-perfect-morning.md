It is quiet when I wake up. And dark. My alarm hasn't gone off yet and the first thing I do when I open my eyes is roll out of bed and dismiss it. I brush my teeth because I'm scared of losing them and because a toothache is universally known to be one of the most mind-boggling types of pain.

I meditate for a little bit because it is good for the mind. Then I do some yoga, counting my breaths and deeply reflecting on my oneness with the universe. I would like to sweat, to get my heart-rate up. To feel alive in some way that pushes my limits. I go for a run on some days, other days, a HIIT workout.

I would really love to learn another language this year, so I do a Duolingo lesson, revise Anki decks, and do some other mental exercises to keep my plastic.

I take a bath, preferably with Adun. My partner deserves to not wake up next to sweaty and smelly, probably. It would be great if I could do all this before 8am.
