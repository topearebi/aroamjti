title: "You Are Nothing, Yet"
date: 2012-04-15 12:00:00 +0000
type: poetry
tags: [love]
---
You are ugly
Inferior in every way
There is nothing special about you
Nothing spectacular
Nothing
You are plain
You are just you
But if these are so
If these are true
Then why do I feel this way about you
Why do I think you more important than all others
Why do I see you alone
Why do you appear special in mine eyes
Where others see nothing in you
I see everything
When they might look upon you but once, before averting eyes
I stop, and stare
Where they see white and black, I see rainbows
Where they see paper, I see cardboard
Where they leaves, I see flowers
Where they see salt, I see sugar
Water, I see honey
Where they see naught, I see you
