You’re 10 when you start to get blackouts. Long periods where you forget who you are. Fate would be so kind to help you forget not remembering things but you learned your lessons about fate at 8 when mother told you your father was no more. Till the next day when you saw daddy sneaking in and back out the door with a box and when you went in to get what you forgot you realised you really are your father’s son and all his things that were there yesterday are gone. Mother tells you not to worry because some day it’ll be as though he never was, but it never is. He never leaves. In your memories he ever lives even when you forget things till you try so hard that you get blackouts. Not a word about him has ever left your mouth.
