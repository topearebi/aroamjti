One day, my little girl said to me
As we were walking down the streets
Of Northumberry
"Why do you stay alive, if you do not value life"
I looked at my little girl
Who was my entire world
Even though my world was so small
And I thought to myself
Spoken like a teenage girl
Who knows so little of the world
Which is exactly what she is

I was like you once
I asked questions of the world
Demanding answers. From no one at all
Some questions are just questions
Problems in their nature
Answers to which, there are none

There are no laws no rules
Yet no flaws and no tools,
Applicable to. The existence of the world
The existence of existence

But I suppose I detract
From the question you ask
The answer to which. Is the answer you seek
And it is

I have little else choice
But to live and fight. Or live a-dying
Because life is dying. And death is the end

But as bleak as it seems. 
These are the only two things, 
We ever get to keep
Forever
Relatively

But now they tell me. 
Now they preach
That all I have
Is not everything
I'll live as long as I'm alive
Die when I'm dead. 
Yet even when I do get to my end
They do not wish to grant me rest

They want to take, 
all I have left
Take my death, 
and give me something else

Is it any wonder then. 
Dear child
That I stick to life. 
And avoid rushing
To an untimely end

I fully intend
To take all I can
From earth and men
Even if in it I find, 
But little fulfillment

And when the time comes
And I'm done and gone
I shall rest my head
On that soiled bed
My lips shall curl
Even as my eyelids fall
Fully satisfied
With what I wrought
On the above

So she looked at me
My little girl
Her huge little eyes
Expectant
Uncomprehending
I squeezed her palm
And bid her calm
"I live for you
Believe its true"
And so we continued
Our walk down the streets
Of Northumberry
