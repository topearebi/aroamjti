Death is a release
A release from life's worries
And it's troubles
In death there is no health, hence no sickness
There are no riches, hence no poverty
No knowledge, hence no stupidity
No emotions, no love, no anger, no hate
No peace, no war
No food, no hunger, no famine
No life, no death
Death is perfect
It is in fact, perfection
But then again, I've never died before
