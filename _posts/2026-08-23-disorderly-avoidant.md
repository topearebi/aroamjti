If it isn't written down, it never happened. And I never have to deal with it. I also have to avoid every person, object or memento from that period to ensure that I never again have to deal with it to make sure. But small price to pay ey?
I've been avoiding a lot of things lately.
I did an interview and didn't get the job. Which sucks because the skil I have most worked on in my journey of living I would argue is being able to have a conversation with 3 or less people. And the reality that I am still not good enough at it really hit like a ton of bricks.
I think my feelings around it revolve around a mix of, I'm not sure I was quite ready for that and I would have been so ace at that.
My avoidance around dealing with this and the reasons and consequences have also partly been aided by my new job. I have barely had time to rest between having places to go and people to have teams meetings with.
I will later come to reflect on time as an interesting concept that I never seem to have enough of no matter how much I have, and yet I continue to somehow underutilise.
I suppose the problem with avoiding dealing with the feelings is, how do I make sure I never feel that way again, if I don't feel it now.
