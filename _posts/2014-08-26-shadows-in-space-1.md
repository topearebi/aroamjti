Walking across planets (cast across the planets)
Heading for the man on the sun (brighter than the sun)
Knowing full well there is none (With no need to burn)
Shadows in space
Dance across my face
I'd really like to touch
But I have none such luck
Shadows in space
Eternally moving
Though I"d rather you stayed with me
Even if not of your choosing
Shadows in space
Formless, without shape
Beautiful even without face
Nothing else can compare
Shadows in space
Travelling through time
Tearing through its fabric of lace
Casually, as if flipping a dime
Shadows in space
Not of the black race
Thou move at own's pace
Unbothered by obstacles in place
Shadows in space
I envy thee
Like a pack of wolves
They devour all
