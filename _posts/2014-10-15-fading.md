I am rusted wings and eyes sunken too deep.
I am winter’s own I am freeze dried bone.
I am rattling knees, gritted gritting teeth.
I am hoarse voice I am raspy breath.
I am slowly shedding tears I am synonymous with death.
I am the footsteps I leave behind me, bits off me run off in the steam of my cold breaths.
I am vaporising, evaporating.
I am disintegrating.
I am fading.
