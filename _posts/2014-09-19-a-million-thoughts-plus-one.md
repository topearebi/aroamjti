I try to write poetry as I lay down in bed
But I just can’t seem to get the words out of my head
The emotions in me are mess
There’s a million thoughts bouncing around in my upstairs
I turn my face upwards
And I’m confronted with a sight too beautiful for words
As I stare at this sky so blue
I wonder how something so chaotic could exist within something so true
I try to seek solace in fate
Try to escape to the earth’s climate
To make wind through me wind
And clear out my mind
But how do I become one with nature
And forget my fear of the future
So all this does is add a thought
To the million I’ve already got

This one’s a mess. But so am I so I might as well put it out there
