She liked boys so she fucked around
Smoked weed so stumbled about
Lost herself in pleasure and peace
Amidst a life others find repulsive
She fell to no man's dictations
Shirked off man's restrictions
She had no friends, what did she need them for
She was happy enough with the things she saw
Needed pay no heed to reality
When she had her dreams
What was ambition to one who could will it all
What was stress to one who but had to think to summon it all
She was happy, she was free
She was mad, she was 18
She had the world within her reach
Not this one but the one she owned
The one that existed solely in her head
