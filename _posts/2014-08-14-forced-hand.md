There are some mistakes you can't help but make
Some actions you can't help but take
Times when you are forced to certain actions
Cornered, to evoke specific reactions
All other choices stripped away
The ones left neither happy nor gay
And you are thereby forced to move
In accordance to a master you cannot prove
But even in these times you do not despair
You forge onward and act as if without care
While being consumed inside by hopelessness
The heart accepting that which the body refuses access
For no matter how tough the skin
Your (heart) is still soft to pin
Though you have fought long and hard
Unaware that your destination is wrong and sad
In the end you shall meet death and sand
For an unknown power has so forced your hand
