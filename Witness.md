It's a Monday again and as always, like new months and new years, it has me thinking of measuring time as one of the many acts of human futility.

I saw something yesterday where someone said that light moves at the speed of causality. Which is very interesting to me for many reasons. One is relativity, how it makes clear why moving faster than light could lead to time travel since you would be faster than causality itself.

This also relates to theories of observation of the universe and objects in it. To the cat in the box which is neither dead or alive until you witness it.

I feel differently about observation and light. I think that observation and causality/existence are not related at all. Perhaps time is static. And everything that has happened and will happen exists at a point in time. Or perhaps it does not

I like to think that the universe is expanding infinitely to observe itself infinitely. Light travels that the universe may witness time.

Perhaps it is the same for us. Perhaps as humans, children born of the causality of the universe. We are here to expand infinitely. To bear witness to our lives.

Somehow, this thought gives me comfort. You can strive to be as big, to live as fully as you wish to bear witness to.
